# physics.ndot.io: published research

This repository mirrors the research published at https://physics.ndot.io. Each folder is one published subproject, exactly as the site shows it: the base problem, every package with its question, all versions of the derivation, the external reviews and the code. A reference folder holds fixed texts that the subprojects cite, such as the definition of the description language.

It is written automatically whenever a subproject is published; changes made here by hand are overwritten.

The formulas are converted to the math syntax of this viewer ($`...`$ and math blocks); the content is unchanged. The canonical rendering, with linked equation numbers, versions and reviews, is at https://physics.ndot.io.

## Reference

### [Ilanguage](00-ilanguage/)

The reference text of the description language Ilanguage, which the subprojects cite. The language describes systems with object-oriented programming patterns and fixes only which states and operations form a valid description; it attaches no interpretation to them.

On the site: https://physics.ndot.io/00-ilanguage/

## Research programme: Gravity in the description language Ilanguage

A research programme on the description language Ilanguage 1.b. It determines which structure of contracts and states is necessary and sufficient for space, time and gravity to be definable inside the language, and examines how typical or how forced that structure is. Its stages are subprojects that build on each other: space, time, bodies in a field, and gravity.

### Stage 1: [Space from a description language](03-ilang-space/)

Can a space-like structure (distance, dimension, locality, bodies, motion) be derived inside the description language Ilanguage 1.b from its own elements alone, without importing any spatial notion? The first stage of a research programme on gravity.

On the site: https://physics.ndot.io/03-ilang-space/

### Stage 2: [Time from a description language](04-ilang-time/)

Can a physical time (clocks, proper time and moving clocks, inertia and internal energy, a common maximal speed, causal order) be derived inside the description language Ilanguage 1.b from its own elements alone, without importing any temporal notion, for bodies in the space derived in 03-ilang-space? The second stage of a research programme on gravity.

On the site: https://physics.ndot.io/04-ilang-time/

### Stage 3: [Clocks and free fall from a description language](05-ilang-free-fall/)

Can a field object act on a body inside the description language Ilanguage 1.b, with the space of 03-ilang-space and the time of 04-ilang-time, so that the rate of the clock the body carries depends on its place and the body falls with an acceleration that does not depend on the body, and are the two tied by a relation that contains no property of the body? The third stage of a research programme on gravity.

On the site: https://physics.ndot.io/05-ilang-free-fall/

### Stage 4: [Bodies as the source of the field, from a description language](06-ilang-source/)

Can bodies that are coupled to a common field object inside the description language Ilanguage 1.b make the field they fall in, so that the field made by one body acts on another as a common local scale, with a clock rate that depends on the place and a fall that does not depend on the body, and with a sign and a dependence on the distance that the description fixes? The fourth stage of a research programme on gravity.

On the site: https://physics.ndot.io/06-ilang-source/

### Stage 5: [Weight, inertia and cost of bodies that make the field, from a description language](07-ilang-weight/)

Can bodies that are coupled to a common field object inside the description language Ilanguage 1.b, with the space of 03-ilang-space, the time of 04-ilang-time, the fall of 05-ilang-free-fall and the field made by bodies of 06-ilang-source, be weighed and resist acceleration in proportion to every one of their costs (the rest cost, the own mark and a bond made through the field), with the same ratio of weight to inertia for all levels and for bound pairs as for a single body, and what must the field, the coupling and the speed with which a change of the field spreads be for that? The fifth stage of a research programme on gravity.

On the site: https://physics.ndot.io/07-ilang-weight/

### Stage 6: [A field with a finite speed that weighs every cost, from a description language](08-ilang-propagation/)

Can one field inside the description language Ilanguage 1.b, with the space of 03-ilang-space, the time of 04-ilang-time, the fall of 05-ilang-free-fall, the field made by bodies of 06-ilang-source and the weight and inertia of 07-ilang-weight, weigh every cost of a body, make a long-range mark and form that mark at a finite speed, all in one description, and what must the field, the grip by which a body holds on to it and the state be for that? In such a field, does the inertia of a body with its own mark follow its cost, in particular when the maximal speed of the bodies equals the speed of the field? The sixth stage of a research programme on gravity.

On the site: https://physics.ndot.io/08-ilang-propagation/
