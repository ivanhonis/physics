## Setup and assumptions

- **Bodies** (assumption): Model D bodies $`b_1,b_2`$ on the same ring of position labels, in clock levels $`k_i`$ with half-gaps $`M_i:=M^{(i)}_{k_i}>0`$, motion amplitudes $`\tau_i`$, each coupled by (a) or (c) with a real constant $`\gamma_i`$ (either sign); no pair term between $`b_1`$ and $`b_2`$; records at $`\kappa\to0`$ (medium terms dropped). Items 1–2: of different types, or of one type (then with equal contract terms). Item 3: of different types (question note (i)), so the labels $`1,2`$ are data. Write $`S_i:=\sigma_3\otimes(m_i\mathbb 1+D_i)`$, $`\Gamma_i`$ the coupling term of $`b_i`$, $`A_i'`$ its motion term ($`A_i`$ for (a); $`A_i`$ plus its $`\gamma_i`$-part for (c)), and
```math
g_i:=\sigma_i\gamma_iM_i ,\qquad \sigma_i\in\{+1,-1\}\ \text{the branch of } b_i.
```
- **Held bodies** (A15): $`\tau_i=0`$ at fixed $`x_1,x_2`$. **Field state** (M9): lowest; highest for even $`L`$ only (question note (ii)).
- **Inputs** (quoted in question.md): (4.2), (4.4) of 06-ilang-source/04-profile ($`M`$, $`M^{(2)}=-\frac{2ng_1g_2}{t_f}\Lambda^+(x_1,x_2)`$, $`\Lambda^+`$ the pseudo-inverse of the ring Laplacian $`\Delta`$); (6.10) of 06-ilang-source/06-own-mark (dressed curvature $`E''_{k,\sigma}(0)`$, error $`O(\gamma^3)`$, under the isolation condition (6.4) there); (7.1), (7.2), (7.7) of 06-ilang-source/07-dressed-fall.
- **Regime of item 3** (assumption, named R):
  - **R1** (isolated band). In each sector of the conserved labels of Step 1, the $`\tau=0`$ group $`\mathcal G=\{e_{jl}\}`$, $`e_{jl}:=\lvert j\,\sigma_1k_1,\ l\,\sigma_2k_2\rangle\otimes\Omega_{jl}`$ ($`\Omega_{jl}`$ the lowest, resp. highest, field state of the held problem with $`b_1`$ at $`j`$, $`b_2`$ at $`l`$), has distance $`\delta_1`$ from the $`\tau=0`$ levels reached by one motion term (one branch flipped) and $`\delta\le\delta_1`$ from all other $`\tau=0`$ levels (same branches with an excited field; both branches flipped). ## Derivation

### Step 1. Conserved quantities (item 1)

Let $`\mathcal T:=T^{(b_1)}T^{(b_2)}\prod_\alpha T^{(\alpha)}`$, $`T\lvert x\rangle=\lvert x+1\rangle`$ on every position label. $`A_i`$, $`B_i`$ and $`C_1`$ are built from $`\sum_x\lvert x+1\rangle\langle x\rvert`$ and $`\sum_x\lvert x\rangle\langle x\rvert`$; under $`\mathcal T`$ the couplings go to $`\sum_xP^{(i)}_{x+1}\otimes S_i\otimes\Phi_{x+1}`$ and the (c) bond factors $`\tfrac12(\Phi_x+\Phi_{x+1})`$ to those of the shifted bond. $`\mathcal T`$ commutes with the swaps of field objects. Hence
```math
[\mathcal T,C]=0,\qquad \mathcal T^L=\mathbb 1,\qquad \mathcal T=e^{-i\hat P},\quad P=p_1+p_2+\textstyle\sum_\alpha q_\alpha\in\tfrac{2\pi}{L}\mathbb Z_L , \qquad\text{(2.1)}
```
the total plane-wave label of bodies and field is conserved. Further: $`C`$ acts on the clock label of $`b_i`$ only through $`\mathbb 1`$ and $`D_i`$, so $`[D_i,C]=0`$ and $`k_i`$ is conserved; the joint reflection $`\mathcal R`$ ($`x\to-x`$ on all labels, times $`\sigma_3`$ on both branch labels) commutes with $`C`$ (the reversal flips the sign of the $`\sigma_1`$ terms, $`\sigma_3`$ flips it back) and $`\mathcal R\mathcal T\mathcal R^\dagger=\mathcal T^{-1}`$, so every level satisfies $`E(P)=E(-P)`$; $`C`$ itself and $`\sum_x\Phi_x=N_f`$ are conserved. **Bodies alone.** For $`\mathcal T_b:=T^{(b_1)}T^{(b_2)}`$, which commutes with $`C_{\rm D}^{(i)}`$ and $`C_1`$,
```math
\mathcal T_bC\mathcal T_b^\dagger-C=\sum_i\gamma_i\sum_xP^{(i)}_x\otimes S_i\otimes(\Phi_{x-1}-\Phi_x)\;+\;(\text{(c): bond terms, off-diagonal in }x_i)\neq0 \qquad\text{(2.2)}
```
whenever $`(\gamma_1,\gamma_2)\ne0`$: $`S_i`$ is invertible ($`m_i>0`$), $`\Phi_{x-1}\neq\Phi_x`$, and terms on different position labels, or diagonal and off-diagonal ones, cannot cancel. So the plane-wave label of the bodies is not conserved (the field takes the recoil). **Sublattice-branch label** ($`L`$ even). $`\Pi_i:=\sum_x(-1)^xP^{(i)}_x`$ anticommutes with every hop of $`b_i`$, the hop across the closing bond included, and $`\sigma_3^{(i)}`$ anticommutes with $`\sigma_1^{(i)}`$; so $`\Pi_i\sigma_3^{(i)}`$ commutes with every term of $`A_i'`$ (the (c) bond factors act on the field). Every other term of $`C`$ is diagonal in $`x_i`$ and carries $`\mathbb 1`$ or $`\sigma_3^{(i)}`$ on the branch label of $`b_i`$. Hence
```math
Q_i:=\Pi_i\,\sigma_3^{(i)},\qquad [Q_i,C]=0\quad(L\ \text{even}),\qquad Q_1e_{jl}=\sigma_1(-1)^je_{jl},\quad Q_2e_{jl}=\sigma_2(-1)^le_{jl}. \qquad\text{(2.17)}
```
A change of $`x_i`$ by an odd number of places goes with a flip of $`\sigma_i`$. For odd $`L`$ the closing bond preserves $`(-1)^x`$ and $`[Q_i,C]\neq0`$. The bodies alone thus conserve $`k_1,k_2`$ and, for even $`L`$, $`Q_1,Q_2`$; held bodies also conserve $`x_i,\sigma_i`$ ($`C`$ is then diagonal in them). **One type.** For two bodies of one type, $`[P_{12},C]=0`$ (equal contract terms), and the labeled $`x_i,D_i,Q_i`$ are not observables (Law 5, M6); the conserved quantities of the bodies alone are their symmetric functions $`D_1+D_2`$, $`D_1D_2`$ and ($`L`$ even) $`Q_1+Q_2`$, $`Q_1Q_2`$, which commute with $`P_{12}`$ and $`C`$; $`\mathcal T`$ and $`\mathcal R`$ commute with $`P_{12}`$.

### Step 2. Held bodies: exact form (item 2)

At $`\tau_i=0`$ couplings (a) and (c) coincide (the (c) addition is proportional to $`\tau_i`$). In the sector $`(x_i,\sigma_i,k_i)`$, $`S_i=\sigma_iM_i`$ and $`C=\sigma_1M_1+\sigma_2M_2+\sum_\alpha h^{(\alpha)}`$ with $`h=C_1+g_1P_{x_1}+g_2P_{x_2}`$, i.e. (7.1) with $`g_b\to g_1`$, $`g_s\to g_2`$. Without pair terms the field eigenvalues are $`\sum_jn_j\varepsilon_j(h)`$, $`\sum_jn_j=N_f`$. By Perron–Frobenius ($`-h`$ irreducible with non-negative off-diagonal entries) $`\varepsilon_0(h)`$ is simple for all real $`g_i`$, hence analytic and connected to $`\gamma_i=0`$:
```math
E(x_1,x_2)=\sigma_1M_1+\sigma_2M_2+N_f\,\varepsilon_0(x_1,x_2)=\mathrm{const}+M(x_1,x_2), \qquad\text{(2.3)}
```
with $`M`$ of (4.2); the one-body terms do not depend on the positions because $`C_1`$ is translation invariant. The ring maps $`x\to x+a`$ and $`x\to x_1+x_2-x`$ leave $`C_1`$ invariant, the second exchanges the two places. Hence, **exactly** (every order in $`g_i`$):
```math
M(x_1,x_2)=\mathcal M(\lvert x_2-x_1\rvert_L),\qquad M(g_1@x_1,g_2@x_2)=M(g_1@x_2,g_2@x_1),\qquad F_1=-F_2 , \qquad\text{(2.4)}
```
where $`F_i:=-\tfrac12[E(x_i+1)-E(x_i-1)]`$ (other label fixed): for $`E=f(x_2-x_1)`$, $`F_1=-\tfrac12[f(d-1)-f(d+1)]=-F_2`$. The force with which $`b_1`$ acts on $`b_2`$ is opposite and equal to that with which $`b_2`$ acts on $`b_1`$. **One type.** For held bodies of one type $`T`$ at $`(x_1,\sigma_1,k_1)\neq(x_2,\sigma_2,k_2)`$, $`P_{12}`$ commutes with $`C`$ and leaves $`h`$ unchanged (it swaps the two point terms together with the bodies), so the admissible eigenvector is $`2^{-1/2}(1+c_TP_{12})\lvert x_1\sigma_1k_1,x_2\sigma_2k_2\rangle\otimes\Omega`$ with the eigenvalue (2.3) (for coinciding entries: the product itself if $`c_T=+1`$, no admissible state if $`c_T=-1`$). So $`E`$ is a function of the set $`\{(x_1,\sigma_1,k_1),(x_2,\sigma_2,k_2)\}`$; (2.4) holds with $`F_i`$ the change when the member $`(x_i,\sigma_i,k_i)`$ of the set is moved, and for equal internal states it reduces to the dependence on $`\lvert x_2-x_1\rvert_L`$: which body acts is then not data. **Highest state** ($`L`$ even). $`U_f:=\prod_\alpha(-1)^{y_\alpha}`$ gives $`U_fC_1U_f=-C_1`$, $`U_f\Phi_xU_f=\Phi_x`$, and $`\sigma_2`$ anticommutes with $`\sigma_1,\sigma_3`$; so $`\mathcal W:=U_f\,\sigma_2^{(1)}\sigma_2^{(2)}`$ satisfies
```math
\mathcal WC\mathcal W^\dagger=-C,\qquad M_{\rm top}(g_1,g_2)=-M_{\rm low}(-g_1,-g_2)\quad(L\ \text{even}). \qquad\text{(2.5)}
```
The $`(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\text{highest})`$ problem is the $`(-\sigma_1,-\sigma_2,\text{lowest})`$ problem with $`C\to-C`$; since $`e^{-iC\lambda}\mathcal W\Psi=\mathcal We^{iC\lambda}\Psi`$ and $`\mathcal W`$ commutes with every $`\hat x_i`$, accelerations from rest are equal.

### Step 3. The mutual term order by order (item 2)

Lowest state (highest: by (2.5)). $`C_1+2t_f=t_f\Delta`$, the lowest one-object state is uniform $`u`$, the gap is $`4t_f\sin^2(\pi/L)`$, and the reduced resolvent is $`R=-\Lambda^+/t_f`$. Rayleigh–Schrödinger perturbation theory in $`V=g_1P_{x_1}+g_2P_{x_2}`$ ($`\lVert V\rVert\le\lvert g_1\rvert+\lvert g_2\rvert`$) converges for $`\lvert g_1\rvert+\lvert g_2\rvert<2t_f\sin^2(\pi/L)`$ (Kato). On the ring ($`D=1`$), with $`u:=d(L-d)`$, $`d\in\{0,\dots,L-1\}`$:
```math
\Lambda^+(d)=\frac{L^2-1}{12L}-\frac{u}{2L},\qquad (\Lambda^+)^2(d)=\frac{(L^2-1)(L^2+11)}{720L}-\frac{u}{12L}-\frac{u^2}{24L}. \qquad\text{(2.6)}
```
Proof: the second differences of $`u`$ and $`u^2`$ are $`-2`$ and $`2L^2+2-12u`$ (use $`(L-2d)^2=L^2-4u`$) for $`d\neq0`$, so $`\Delta`$ maps the right-hand sides to $`\delta_{d0}-\frac1L`$ and to $`\Lambda^+(d)`$; the constants give zero mean ($`\sum_du=L(L^2-1)/6`$, $`\sum_du^2=L(L^4-1)/30`$). With (4.4):
```math
M^{(2)}(d)=-\frac{2n}{t_f}g_1g_2\Lambda^+(d)=\frac{n\,g_1g_2}{6t_fL}\bigl[6u-(L^2-1)\bigr]. \qquad\text{(2.7)}
```
Third order: $`E^{(3)}=\langle u\vert VRVRV\vert u\rangle-E^{(1)}\langle u\vert VR^2V\vert u\rangle`$, $`V\lvert u\rangle=L^{-1/2}\sum_ig_i\lvert x_i\rangle`$, $`E^{(1)}=(g_1+g_2)/L`$. Keeping the terms containing both $`g_1`$ and $`g_2`$ and using (2.6) ($`\Lambda_d:=\Lambda^+(d)`$, $`(\Lambda^2)_d:=(\Lambda^+)^2(d)`$):
```math
M^{(3)}(d)=\frac{n}{t_f^2}g_1g_2(g_1+g_2)\Bigl[\Lambda_d^2+2\Lambda_0\Lambda_d-\tfrac{(\Lambda^2)_0+2(\Lambda^2)_d}{L}\Bigr]=\frac{n\,g_1g_2(g_1+g_2)}{60\,t_f^2L^2}\bigl[20u^2-10(L^2-2)u+(L^2-1)(L^2-4)\bigr]. \qquad\text{(2.8)}
```
**Product form.** At second order $`M^{(2)}=g_1\cdot g_2\cdot F_2(d)`$, $`F_2=-2n\Lambda^+/t_f`$: one number per body ($`g_i`$, the same for acting and for being acted upon) times one function of $`d`$, for every pair of the class. At third order it fails:
```math
\nexists\ p_1(g_1),\,p_2(g_2),\,F(d):\quad M=p_1p_2F(d)+O(g^4)\ \text{for all }g_1,g_2\text{ near }0 . \qquad\text{(2.9)}
```
Proof: such a form makes $`M(d)/M(0)=F(d)/F(0)`$ independent of $`g_i`$ ($`M^{(2)}(0)\ne0`$ since $`\Lambda_0>0`$). Expanding, the $`O(g_1+g_2)`$ term of $`M(d)/M(0)`$ vanishes only if $`B_3(d)\Lambda_0=B_3(0)\Lambda_d`$ for all $`d`$, with $`B_3`$ the bracket of (2.8). $`B_3`$ is quadratic in $`u`$ with leading coefficient $`1/(3L^2)\neq0`$, $`\Lambda`$ is linear in $`u`$, and $`u`$ takes the three distinct values $`0,L-1,2L-4`$ at $`d=0,1,2`$ ($`L\ge5`$): impossible. (Among pairs with $`g_1g_2\neq0`$, $`M^{(3)}\equiv0`$ only for $`g_1+g_2=0`$.)
**Controls.** (2.4) fails in the wider class with a non-uniform on-site field term $`\sum_xv_x\Phi_x`$: the one-body terms then depend on $`x_i`$ and $`F_1+F_2\neq0`$. The second-order product form has no control in the class ((2.7) holds for all its members); its failure at third order is (2.9).

### Step 4. Band dynamics of the free bodies (item 3)

By R1 there is a Schrieffer–Wolff unitary $`\mathcal U=e^{S}`$, $`S=-S^\dagger`$, built from $`C`$, such that $`H:=\mathcal U^\dagger C\mathcal U`$ is block diagonal with respect to the projector $`\mathcal P_0`$ on $`\mathcal G`$; the band is $`\mathcal U\,\mathrm{Ran}\,\mathcal P_0`$ (standard). Its first-order part, $`O(\tau'/\delta_1)`$, connects $`\mathcal G`$ to the one-flip levels; the levels at distance $`\ge\delta`$ are reached only at second order, with couplings $`O(\tau'^2/\delta_1)\ll\delta`$, and their elimination is a relative correction $`O(\tau'^2/(\delta_1\delta))`$ (R2). $`\mathcal U`$ commutes with $`\mathcal T`$ and, for even $`L`$, with $`Q_i`$. In the basis $`e_{jl}`$:
```math
H_{\rm eff}=\mathcal P_0H\mathcal P_0=W+K_1+K_2+O(\tau^4),\qquad W=\mathrm{diag}\,E(j,l)\ \text{of (2.3)}. \qquad\text{(2.10)}
```
$`\mathcal P_0A_i'\mathcal P_0=0`$ because $`A_i'\propto\sigma_1`$ flips $`\sigma_i`$; so the first-order part is $`W`$, and $`K_i`$, the second-order term of $`A_i'`$, moves $`b_i`$ by $`r\in\{0,\pm2\}`$ and leaves the other body fixed (a product $`A_1'A_2'`$ flips both branches and leaves $`\mathcal G`$); for even $`L`$, by (2.17), every term of $`H_{\rm eff}`$ moves each body by an even number of places. In the real gauge $`K_1`$ is real symmetric; let $`\kappa(j,l):=\langle e_{j+1,l}\vert K_1\vert e_{j-1,l}\rangle=\bar\kappa+\delta\kappa(j,l)`$, where $`\bar\kappa`$ is the same amplitude for $`b_1`$ alone with the field and $`\delta\kappa\to0`$ as $`g_2\to0`$. For $`b_1`$ alone the Bloch sums $`\sum_je^{iPj}e_j`$ have total label $`P`$ (2.1), so its dressed level is $`E_{k_1,\sigma_1}(P)=\sum_r\bar\kappa_re^{-iPr}`$ with $`r\in\{0,\pm2\}`$ at order $`\tau^2`$, and
```math
E''_{k_1,\sigma_1}(0)=-\textstyle\sum_rr^2\bar\kappa_r=-8\bar\kappa , \qquad\text{(2.11)}
```
the curvature (6.10), which applies by the single-body part of R1. The same holds for $`b_2`$. For the highest state ($`L`$ even) the gauge gives $`\mathcal Te_j=(-1)^{N_f}e_{j+1}`$, which shifts $`P`$ by $`\pi N_f`$ but does not change (2.11) since $`r`$ is even, and by (2.5) $`E''_{\rm top,\sigma}=-E''_{{\rm low},-\sigma}`$, of sign $`\sigma`$.

### Step 5. Mean positions and accelerations from rest (item 3)

**Mean position.** For body $`i`$ let $`\chi_i(x)`$ be the representative of $`x\in\mathbb Z_L`$ in the window $`\{c_i-\lfloor L/2\rfloor,\dots,c_i-\lfloor L/2\rfloor+L-1\}`$ around its chart centre $`c_i`$ (R3), and let the *cut bond* $`\beta_i`$ join the last place $`x_i^*`$ of the window to $`x_i^*+1`$. The mean position is $`\langle\hat x_i\rangle`$, $`\hat x_i:=\sum_x\chi_i(x)P^{(i)}_x`$: a diagonal self-adjoint operator on the places of $`b_i`$, well defined for distinguishable bodies and independent of the common phase. Let $`C(\theta)`$ be $`C`$ with every term that moves $`b_i`$ one place forward multiplied by $`e^{-i\theta}`$ (backward: $`e^{i\theta}`$), $`v_i:=\partial_\theta C(\theta)\vert_{0}`$ the *winding current* of $`b_i`$, and $`v_{i*}`$ its part on $`\beta_i`$. Since $`\chi_i(x+1)-\chi_i(x)=1`$ except $`1-L`$ on $`\beta_i`$, and $`\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm d\lambda}\langle O\rangle=\langle i[C,O]\rangle`$ by (1.9), exactly:
```math
i[C,\hat x_i]=v_i-L\,v_{i*},\qquad \frac{\mathrm d^2}{\mathrm d\lambda^2}\langle\hat x_i\rangle=\langle i[C,v_i]\rangle-L\,\langle i[C,v_{i*}]\rangle . \qquad\text{(2.18)}
```
$`[C,v_{i*}]`$ acts on $`b_i`$ only at the four places within one place of $`\beta_i`$, so the cut term vanishes for states without weight there. We write $`a_i:=\frac{\mathrm d^2}{\mathrm d\lambda^2}\langle\hat x_i\rangle\vert_0`$; by the gauge remark of the Setup the start is at rest.

**Band evaluation.** $`C(\theta)`$ has the same $`\tau=0`$ part as $`C`$, so Step 4 applies to it: $`C(\theta)=\mathcal U(\theta)H(\theta)\mathcal U(\theta)^\dagger`$, and with $`Z:=\mathcal U^\dagger\partial_\theta\mathcal U`$, $`v_i=\mathcal U(\partial_\theta H+[Z,H])\mathcal U^\dagger`$. As $`H`$ is block diagonal and $`\psi\in\mathrm{Ran}\,\mathcal P_0`$, $`\langle i[C,v_1]\rangle=\langle\psi\vert i[H_{\rm eff},\partial_\theta H_{\rm eff}]\vert\psi\rangle+\langle\psi\vert i[H_{\rm eff},[\zeta,H_{\rm eff}]]\vert\psi\rangle`$ with $`\zeta:=\mathcal P_0Z\mathcal P_0`$. In $`H_{\rm eff}(\theta)`$ a term that moves $`b_1`$ by $`r`$ places, every hop counted, carries $`e^{-ir\theta}`$, so $`\partial_\theta H_{\rm eff}=-i\sum_rrK_{1,r}`$. The first-order part of $`S(\theta)`$ is $`e^{-i\theta}S_++e^{i\theta}S_-+S_2`$ ($`S_\pm`$ moving $`b_1`$ by $`\pm1`$, $`S_2`$ moving $`b_2`$): $`\mathcal P_0\partial_\theta S\mathcal P_0=0`$, and $`\mathcal P_0[S,\partial_\theta S]\mathcal P_0\propto\mathcal P_0[S_+,S_-]\mathcal P_0`$ moves nobody ($`[S_\pm,S_2]`$ flips both branches), so $`\zeta`$ is diagonal in $`e_{jl}`$ and $`O(\tau^2/\delta_1^2)`$, and its term is a relative $`O(\tau'^2/\delta_1^2)`$ correction. The cut term is the same with the twist on $`\beta_1`$ alone, i.e. with $`r`$ replaced by the signed number of crossings of $`\beta_1`$. Together, a hop of $`K_1`$ from $`j-1`$ to $`j+1`$ enters with the chart displacement $`\Delta\chi_1(j):=\chi_1(j+1)-\chi_1(j-1)`$, which is $`2`$, except $`2-L`$ for the two places $`j\in\{x_1^*,x_1^*+1\}`$ next to the cut. The $`W`$-part gives $`4\sum_{j,l}\Delta\chi_1(j)\,\kappa\,\phi^1_{j+1}\phi^1_{j-1}(\phi^2_l)^2\,\partial_1W`$, and at first order in the place dependence $`\kappa\to\bar\kappa=-E''_1/8`$ (2.11). The $`K`$-part vanishes for the winding current at this order ($`[K_1,\sum_rrK_{1,r}]=0`$ for $`\kappa=\bar\kappa`$), but not for the cut: with $`K_1=\bar\kappa(T_1^2+T_1^{-2})+\mathrm{const}`$ and $`T_{1*}^{2}`$ the part of $`T_1^2`$ that crosses $`\beta_1`$, it is $`-L\bar\kappa^2\langle\psi\vert[T_1^2+T_1^{-2},T_{1*}^2-T_{1*}^{-2}]\vert\psi\rangle`$, evaluated term by term. Hence
```math
a_1=a_1^{(0)}-E''_{k_1,\sigma_1}(0)\sum_{j,l}\tfrac12\Delta\chi_1(j)\,\phi^1_{j+1}\phi^1_{j-1}(\phi^2_l)^2\,\partial_1W(j,l),\qquad a_1^{(0)}=-\frac{L}{64}\bigl(E''_{k_1,\sigma_1}(0)\bigr)^2\,\Xi_1 , \qquad\text{(2.12)}
```
with $`\Xi_1:=2\bigl[\phi_{a-1}\phi_{a+3}+\phi_a\phi_{a+4}-\phi_{a-3}\phi_{a+1}-\phi_{a-2}\phi_{a+2}+\phi_{a-1}^2+\phi_a^2-\phi_{a+1}^2-\phi_{a+2}^2\bigr]`$ at $`\phi=\phi^1`$, $`a=x_1^*`$, and $`a_2`$ likewise (with $`\phi^1\leftrightarrow\phi^2`$, $`\partial_2W`$, $`\Delta\chi_2`$, $`\Xi_2`$). Here $`\partial_1W(j,l):=\tfrac12[W(j+1,l)-W(j-1,l)]`$, $`\partial_2`$ likewise, $`W`$ the exact held eigenvalue (2.3) (lowest or, $`L`$ even, highest), and $`\partial_2W=-\partial_1W=\mathcal M'(l-j):=\tfrac12[\mathcal M(d+1)-\mathcal M(d-1)]`$ by (2.4). The sum is of order $`\tau_i^2`$ with the relative errors of R2. $`a_i^{(0)}`$ does not involve the other body: it is the zeroth-order (free-packet) part, of order $`\tau_i^4`$, from the spreading of the packet across $`\beta_i`$; we call $`a_i-a_i^{(0)}`$ the *mutual part*. If $`\phi^i`$ vanishes on the four places within one place of $`\beta_i`$, then $`a_i^{(0)}=0`$, $`\tfrac12\Delta\chi_i=1`$ on every non-zero summand, and (2.12) coincides with the formula for the winding-current acceleration $`\langle i[C,v_i]\rangle`$.

### Step 6. Ratio, inertias, rest costs and directions (item 3)

For real Gaussian envelopes $`\phi^i_j\propto e^{-(\chi_i(j)-c_i)^2/4w_i^2}`$ (width $`w_i\ll L`$), $`\phi^i_{j+1}\phi^i_{j-1}=e^{-1/2w_i^2}(\phi^i_j)^2`$ for every $`j`$ except the two places next to $`\beta_i`$. So the mutual parts in (2.12) are $`\mp e^{-1/2w_i^2}\sum_{j,l}(\phi^1_j\phi^2_l)^2\mathcal M'(l-j)`$ up to the terms of those places, and
```math
\frac{a_1-a_1^{(0)}}{a_2-a_2^{(0)}}=-\frac{E''_{k_1,\sigma_1}(0)}{E''_{k_2,\sigma_2}(0)}\,e^{(w_2^{-2}-w_1^{-2})/2},\qquad w_1=w_2:\ \ \frac{a_1-a_1^{(0)}}{a_2-a_2^{(0)}}=-\frac{\mu_2}{\mu_1},\quad \mu_i:=\frac1{E''_{k_i,\sigma_i}(0)} . \qquad\text{(2.13)}
```
This holds for the exact $`M`$ (every order in $`g_i`$) and to the order to which $`E''`$ is known ((6.10): $`O(\gamma^3)`$), up to relative corrections at most of order $`L\varpi_i\max\lvert\mathcal M'\rvert/\lvert\sum_{j,l}(\phi^1_j\phi^2_l)^2\mathcal M'\rvert`$, $`\varpi_i:=\sum_{j\in\{x_i^*,x_i^*+1\}}\lvert\phi^i_{j+1}\phi^i_{j-1}\rvert`$, from the places next to the cuts (the summands there carry $`\tfrac12\Delta\chi_i=1-L/2`$ and violate the Gaussian identity). With $`a_i^{(0)}=0`$ (no envelope weight next to the cuts) it reads $`\mu_1a_1+\mu_2a_2=0`$. For other envelopes the factor is the ratio of the weighted sums of (2.12). **Rest costs.** Let $`R_i`$ be half the difference of the $`\tau=0`$ eigenvalues of $`b_i`$ alone in its two branches (field lowest), a difference of eigenvalues (M6): $`R_i=M_i+\tfrac{N_f}2[\varepsilon_0(\gamma_iM_i)-\varepsilon_0(-\gamma_iM_i)]=M_i(1+\gamma_in)+O(\gamma_i^3)`$, since the odd part of $`\varepsilon_0(g)`$ is $`g/L+O(g^3)`$. From (6.10), $`\frac1{1-x+x^2}=1+x+O(x^3)`$ and $`\Delta''=O(\gamma^2)`$:
```math
\mu_i=\sigma_i\frac{R_i}{\tau_i^2}+O(\gamma_i^2)\ \ \text{(a)},\qquad \mu_i=\sigma_i\frac{R_i}{\tau_i^2(1+\gamma_in)^2}+O(\gamma_i^2)\ \ \text{(c)}, \qquad\text{(2.14)}
```
so for $`w_1=w_2`$ the ratio of the mutual parts is $`-\sigma_1\sigma_2\,(R_2/\tau_2^2)/(R_1/\tau_1^2)\,[1+O(\gamma^2)]`$ for (a), with $`\tau_i\to\tau_i(1+\gamma_in)`$ for (c). **Directions.** By (2.7), for $`1\le d\le L-1`$, $`\mathcal M'(d)=\frac{ng_1g_2}{t_fL}(L-2d)`$ at second order (exact central difference of a quadratic), $`\mathcal M'(0)=0`$. Take $`c_1<c_2`$ with the separations concentrated in $`0<d<L/2`$ (shorter arc). Then the mutual parts are $`E''_1\langle\mathcal M'\rangle_1`$ and $`-E''_2\langle\mathcal M'\rangle_2`$ with $`\langle\mathcal M'\rangle_i`$ of the sign of $`g_1g_2=\sigma_1\sigma_2\gamma_1\gamma_2M_1M_2`$, and $`\mathrm{sgn}E''_i=\sigma_i`$ for small $`\gamma_in`$ (6.10). Hence, at second order in the couplings, for the mutual parts:
```math
\text{lowest state: towards each other}\iff\sigma_1=\sigma_2=\mathrm{sgn}(\gamma_1\gamma_2);\quad\text{apart}\iff\sigma_1=\sigma_2=-\mathrm{sgn}(\gamma_1\gamma_2);\quad \sigma_1\neq\sigma_2:\ \text{same direction} , \qquad\text{(2.15)}
```
and for the highest state ($`L`$ even) the same with $`\sigma_i\to-\sigma_i`$, by (2.5).

### Step 7. Where the ratio deviates from the ratio of inertias (item 3)

At first order in the place dependence (2.13) holds for equal widths. The first coupling-dependent deviation is $`\delta\kappa`$, the change of $`b_1`$'s hopping caused by $`b_2`$; it multiplies $`\partial W`$ and is thus of second order in the place dependence.

**Exact form of $`\kappa`$** (order $`\tau^2`$, both couplings, lowest or highest state). Let $`\mathcal C_f:=\sum_\alpha C_1^{(\alpha)}`$, $`\mu:=2\sigma_1M_1`$, $`\Omega_\pm:=\Omega_{j\pm1,l}`$, $`F_\pm:=\mathcal C_f+g_1\Phi_{j\pm1}+g_2\Phi_l`$ with $`F_\pm\Omega_\pm=\mathcal E_\pm\Omega_\pm`$, and $`F_0:=\mathcal C_f-g_1\Phi_j+g_2\Phi_l`$ the field contract of the intermediate level ($`b_1`$ at $`j`$ in branch $`-\sigma_1`$). Then $`F_0=F_\pm-V_\pm`$, $`V_\pm:=g_1(\Phi_j+\Phi_{j\pm1})`$, and the (c) bond factor of the hop between $`j`$ and $`j\pm1`$ is $`\hat B_\pm:=1+\tfrac{\gamma_1}2(\Phi_j+\Phi_{j\pm1})=1+V_\pm/\mu`$. The symmetrized second-order Schrieffer–Wolff term, summed over all field states of the intermediate level, is
```math
\kappa(j,l)=\kappa_0\,\tfrac{\mu}{2}\langle\Omega_+\vert B_+(G_++G_-)B_-\vert\Omega_-\rangle,\qquad G_\pm:=(\mu+\mathcal E_\pm-F_0)^{-1},\quad \lVert G_\pm\rVert\le1/\delta_1\ \text{(R1)},
```
with $`B_\pm=1`$ for (a), $`B_\pm=\hat B_\pm`$ for (c), and $`\kappa_0`$ the product of the two hop amplitudes of $`A_1`$ divided by $`\mu`$, the same for all $`(j,l)`$ and for $`b_1`$ alone. The eigenvalue equations give exactly $`(\mu+\mathcal E_\pm-F_0)\Omega_\pm=\mu\hat B_\pm\Omega_\pm`$ and $`(\mu+\mathcal E_\mp-F_0)\Omega_\pm=(\mu\pm\Delta+V_\pm)\Omega_\pm`$, $`\Delta:=\mathcal E_--\mathcal E_+=2\mathcal M'(l-j)`$; so $`G_\pm\hat B_\pm\Omega_\pm=\Omega_\pm/\mu`$ and $`G_\mp\Omega_\pm=(\Omega_\pm-G_\mp V_\pm\Omega_\pm)/(\mu\pm\Delta)`$ ($`G_\pm`$ real symmetric). With $`\langle X\rangle:=\langle\Omega_+\vert X\vert\Omega_-\rangle`$ this gives, for $`\lvert\Delta\rvert<2M_1`$,
```math
\frac{\kappa(j,l)}{\kappa_0}=\langle1\rangle\mp\frac{\langle V_++V_-\rangle}{2\mu}+\rho,\qquad \rho=0\ \text{(c)},\qquad \rho=\sum_\pm\frac{\pm\Delta\langle V_\mp\rangle/\mu+\langle V_+G_\mp V_-\rangle}{2(\mu\pm\Delta)}\ \text{(a)},
```
upper sign (a), lower (c), and $`\frac{\langle V_++V_-\rangle}{2\mu}=\frac{\gamma_1}4\langle\Phi_{j-1}+2\Phi_j+\Phi_{j+1}\rangle`$. No denominator is expanded: with $`\nu:=\max_\pm\lVert(\Phi_j+\Phi_{j\pm1})\Omega_\pm\rVert\le2N_f`$, $`\lvert\rho\rvert\le\frac{\lvert g_1\rvert\nu}{2M_1-\lvert\Delta\rvert}\bigl(\frac{\lvert\Delta\rvert}{2M_1}+\frac{\lvert g_1\rvert\nu}{\delta_1}\bigr)`$. The condition $`\lvert\Delta\rvert<2M_1`$ holds if $`\lvert\gamma_1\rvert N_f<2`$ ($`\lvert\Delta\rvert\le\lVert F_+-F_-\rVert\le\lvert g_1\rvert N_f`$), and whenever the place dependence is small against $`M_1`$.

**Place dependence.** At $`g_2=0`$ the right-hand side is $`\bar\kappa/\kappa_0=1\mp\gamma_1n+O(\epsilon^2)`$, where $`\epsilon`$ is the common scale $`\gamma_i\to\epsilon\gamma_i`$ at fixed $`M_i,t_f,L,N_f`$. The $`l`$-dependent parts are: (i) $`\langle1\rangle=1-\tfrac12\lVert\Omega_+-\Omega_-\rVert^2`$ (real normalized vectors), with $`\Omega_+-\Omega_-=O(g_1)`$, so $`O(g_1^2g_2)`$; (ii) as $`g_1\to0`$, $`\Omega_\pm\to`$ the field state made by $`b_2`$ alone, whose density is $`n+\delta\rho_l(y)`$, so $`\frac{\langle V_++V_-\rangle}{2\mu}=\gamma_1[n+\overline{\delta\rho}_l(j)]`$ plus a correction whose $`l`$-dependent part is $`O(\gamma_1g_1g_2)`$; (iii) in $`\rho`$, the $`\Delta`$-terms are $`O(\gamma_1\nu\mathcal M'/M_1)`$ and vanish at $`g_2=0`$, and the $`l`$-dependent part of the $`G`$-terms is $`O\bigl(\frac{g_1^2\lvert g_2\rvert}{M_1\delta_1}(\frac1{\delta_1}+\frac1{\delta_f})\bigr)`$ (from $`\partial_{g_2}G_\pm`$ and $`\partial_{g_2}\Omega_\pm`$); (iv) the division by $`\bar\kappa/\kappa_0`$ contributes $`O(\gamma_1^2\overline{\delta\rho})`$. In (i), (ii) and (iii), $`\Omega_{jl}`$ is the simple lowest (highest) field state of the held problem (Step 2), and analytic perturbation theory in $`g_1,g_2`$ bounds the coefficients by inverse powers of its field gap $`\delta_f\ge\delta`$ (R1). Each of (i)–(iv) is $`O(\epsilon^3)`$. Hence
```math
\frac{\delta\kappa(j,l)}{\bar\kappa}=\mp\gamma_1\,\overline{\delta\rho}_l(j)+r_1(j,l),\qquad \overline{\delta\rho}_l(j):=\tfrac14[\delta\rho_l(j-1)+2\delta\rho_l(j)+\delta\rho_l(j+1)],\qquad \gamma_1\delta\rho_l(y)=\frac{\sigma_1M^{(2)}(y,l)}{M_1}+O(\gamma_1g_2^2),\qquad r_1=O(\epsilon^3), \qquad\text{(2.16)}
```
upper sign (a), lower (c). The leading term is $`O(\epsilon^2)`$; $`r_1`$ collects (i)–(iv); its constants depend on $`\delta_f`$, for (a) also on $`\delta_1`$ (and on $`t_f,L,N_f`$), and relative to the leading term it is $`O(\epsilon)`$. The last identity: $`\delta\rho_l(y)=-\frac{2ng_2}{t_f}\Lambda^+(y-l)+O(g_2^2)`$, so $`g_1\delta\rho_l=M^{(2)}+O(g_1g_2^2)`$; highest state likewise with $`M^{(2)}_{\rm top}`$. With $`s_i:=+1`$ for (a) and $`-1`$ for (c), the summand of the mutual part of $`a_i`$ in (2.12) acquires the factor $`1-s_i\frac{\sigma_i}{M_i}\bar M^{(2)}(l-j)+r_i(j,l)`$, $`\bar M^{(2)}(d):=\tfrac14[M^{(2)}(d-1)+2M^{(2)}(d)+M^{(2)}(d+1)]`$, where $`r_i=O(\epsilon^3)`$ is the remainder of (2.16) for body $`i`$ together with the error of its last identity. For equal-width Gaussian envelopes the common envelope factor cancels, and with $`p_{jl}:=(\phi^1_j\phi^2_l)^2`$:
```math
\frac{a_1-a_1^{(0)}}{a_2-a_2^{(0)}}=-\frac{\mu_2}{\mu_1}\Bigl[1-\langle\!\langle\bar M^{(2)}\rangle\!\rangle\Bigl(\frac{s_1\sigma_1}{M_1}-\frac{s_2\sigma_2}{M_2}\Bigr)+\langle\!\langle r_1-r_2\rangle\!\rangle+O\bigl((\bar M^{(2)}/M_i)^2\bigr)\Bigr],\qquad \langle\!\langle f\rangle\!\rangle:=\frac{\sum_{j,l}p_{jl}\,f(l-j)\,\mathcal M'(l-j)}{\sum_{j,l}p_{jl}\,\mathcal M'(l-j)} . \qquad\text{(2.19)}
```
Its errors are separate: (1) the coupling-order remainder $`\langle\!\langle r_1-r_2\rangle\!\rangle=O(\epsilon^3)`$, at most $`\max\lvert r_1-r_2\rvert\sum p\lvert\mathcal M'\rvert/\lvert\sum p\mathcal M'\rvert`$ (the last factor is $`1`$ when $`\mathcal M'`$ has one sign on the support, e.g. at second order on the shorter arc), with the gap-dependent constants of (2.16); (2) the ratio expansion, $`O((\bar M^{(2)}/M_i)^2)=O(\epsilon^4)`$; (3) the finite-$`\tau`$ errors of R2; (4) the cut corrections of (2.13). The force-weighted average $`\langle\!\langle\bar M^{(2)}\rangle\!\rangle`$ is not in general the value at the mean separation. The deviation is of relative order $`\gamma_1\gamma_2`$; its leading term vanishes iff $`s_1\sigma_1/M_1=s_2\sigma_2/M_2`$ or $`\langle\!\langle\bar M^{(2)}\rangle\!\rangle=0`$, and then the coupling-order deviation is $`O(\epsilon^3)`$. Each body's inertia is changed in place by the other's mark. **Controls** of (2.13): unequal widths (factor $`e^{(w_2^{-2}-w_1^{-2})/2}`$), and (2.19).

## Result

- **(2.1), (2.2), (2.17)** [class: both couplings, all $`N_f\ge1`$, $`L\ge5`$; exact] Conserved: the total plane-wave label $`P`$ of bodies and field (joint translation $`\mathcal T`$), the clock levels $`k_1,k_2`$ ($`D_1,D_2`$), $`C`$, $`\sum_x\Phi_x`$, and for even $`L`$ the sublattice-branch labels $`Q_i=(\sum_x(-1)^xP^{(i)}_x)\sigma_3^{(i)}`$; the joint reflection maps $`P\to-P`$. The plane-wave label of the bodies alone is not conserved for $`(\gamma_1,\gamma_2)\ne0`$; held bodies also conserve $`x_i,\sigma_i`$. For bodies of one type the conserved quantities of the bodies alone are $`D_1+D_2`$, $`D_1D_2`$ and (even $`L`$) $`Q_1+Q_2`$, $`Q_1Q_2`$.
- **(2.3)–(2.4)** [held bodies, both couplings, lowest state, and highest state for even $`L`$; exact, every order in $`g_i`$] The eigenvalue is $`\sigma_1M_1+\sigma_2M_2+N_f\varepsilon_0`$; it depends on the positions only through the ring distance, is unchanged when the two bodies exchange places, and $`F_1=-F_2`$ (action equals reaction). For bodies of one type it is a function of the set of the two held bodies, and (2.4) holds for the members of that set.
- **(2.5)** [$`L`$ even, exact] $`\mathcal WC\mathcal W^\dagger=-C`$: the highest field state with branches $`(\sigma_1,\sigma_2)`$ is the lowest with $`(-\sigma_1,-\sigma_2)`$ and $`C\to-C`$; $`M_{\rm top}(g_1,g_2)=-M_{\rm low}(-g_1,-g_2)`$.
- **(2.6)–(2.8)** [ring, lowest state, $`\lvert g_1\rvert+\lvert g_2\rvert<2t_f\sin^2(\pi/L)`$] Closed forms of $`\Lambda^+`$, $`(\Lambda^+)^2`$, $`M^{(2)}`$ and $`M^{(3)}`$.
- **(2.9)** [same class] At second order the mutual term is a product $`g_1g_2F_2(d)`$ (yes); at third order no product $`p_1(g_1)p_2(g_2)F(d)`$ exists (no); $`M^{(3)}=0`$ for pairs with $`g_1+g_2=0`$.
- **(2.18)** [both couplings, bodies of different types, every state; exact] The chart mean position $`\langle\hat x_i\rangle`$ has the second derivative $`\langle i[C,v_i]\rangle-L\langle i[C,v_{i*}]\rangle`$: the acceleration of the winding current plus a cut term that acts only on the four places within one place of the cut bond.
- **(2.11)–(2.12)** [regime R1–R3, both couplings, bodies of different types, lowest or (even $`L`$) highest state; mutual part at order $`\tau_i^2`$ and first order in the place dependence, relative errors $`O(\tau'^2/\delta_1^2)+O(\tau'^2/(\delta_1\delta))`$] The drift acceleration from rest of the chart mean position is $`a_i=a_i^{(0)}-E''_{k_i,\sigma_i}(0)\times`$ the envelope-weighted symmetric gradient of the exact held eigenvalue with the chart weights $`\tfrac12\Delta\chi_i`$, with $`E''`$ the curvature (6.10) of the isolated dressed body; the free-packet part $`a_i^{(0)}=-\frac{L}{64}E''^2_i\Xi_i`$ (leading order, $`\tau_i^4`$) vanishes for envelopes without weight on the four places next to the cut, and then $`\tfrac12\Delta\chi_i=1`$ on every non-zero summand.
- **(2.13)–(2.14)** [same, real Gaussian envelopes of widths $`w_i\ll L`$, $`\sum_{j,l}(\phi^1_j\phi^2_l)^2\mathcal M'(l-j)\neq0`$; up to relative corrections at most of order $`L\varpi_i\max\lvert\mathcal M'\rvert/\lvert\sum p\mathcal M'\rvert`$ from the places next to the cuts] $`(a_1-a_1^{(0)})/(a_2-a_2^{(0)})=-(E''_1/E''_2)e^{(w_2^{-2}-w_1^{-2})/2}`$; for equal widths it is $`-\mu_2/\mu_1`$, for every order in $`g_i`$ of the mutual term and to the order of (6.10); $`\mu_i=\sigma_iR_i/\tau_i^2+O(\gamma_i^2)`$ for (a), $`\mu_i=\sigma_iR_i/[\tau_i(1+\gamma_in)]^2+O(\gamma_i^2)`$ for (c), $`R_i`$ the rest cost.
- **(2.15)** [same, second order in the couplings, small $`\gamma_in`$, separations on the shorter arc; mutual parts] Lowest state: towards each other iff $`\sigma_1=\sigma_2=\mathrm{sgn}(\gamma_1\gamma_2)`$, apart iff $`\sigma_1=\sigma_2=-\mathrm{sgn}(\gamma_1\gamma_2)`$, same direction iff $`\sigma_1\ne\sigma_2`$; highest state ($`L`$ even): $`\sigma_i\to-\sigma_i`$.
- **(2.16), (2.19)** [same; second order in the place dependence, equal-width Gaussian envelopes; under $`\gamma_i\to\epsilon\gamma_i`$ leading order $`\epsilon^2`$, remainder $`O(\epsilon^3)`$ with constants depending on the field gap $`\delta_f\ge\delta`$ and, for (a), on $`\delta_1`$] The hopping of each body is changed by the other's mark by the relative amount $`\mp\gamma_i\overline{\delta\rho}+r_i`$, $`r_i=O(\epsilon^3)`$ (upper sign (a), lower (c)); the ratio of the mutual parts deviates from $`-\mu_2/\mu_1`$ by the relative amount $`-\langle\!\langle\bar M^{(2)}\rangle\!\rangle(s_1\sigma_1/M_1-s_2\sigma_2/M_2)`$, of order $`\gamma_1\gamma_2`$, with the force-weighted average $`\langle\!\langle\cdot\rangle\!\rangle`$, up to the coupling-order remainder $`\langle\!\langle r_1-r_2\rangle\!\rangle=O(\epsilon^3)`$, the ratio-expansion error $`O((\bar M^{(2)}/M_i)^2)`$, the finite-$`\tau`$ errors of R2 and the cut corrections of (2.13).

## Consistency checks

1. **Dimensions.** $`C,\tau_i,M_i,t_f,g_i\sim\lambda^{-1}`$; $`\gamma_i,n,\Lambda^+,\chi_i,\Xi_i`$ dimensionless; $`M^{(2)}\sim g^2/t_f`$, $`M^{(3)}\sim g^3/t_f^2\sim\lambda^{-1}`$; $`E''\sim\tau^2/M\sim\lambda^{-1}`$, so both terms of $`a_i`$ in (2.12) are $`\sim\lambda^{-2}`$, as $`\mathrm d^2\langle\hat x_i\rangle/\mathrm d\lambda^2`$.
2. **Held source and test body.** For $`\tau_2\to0`$, $`\phi^2_l=\delta_{l,x_s}`$ and $`\phi^1`$ without weight next to the cut, (2.12) is (7.7) with $`W`$ of (7.2), $`g_s=g_2`$. For $`\gamma_1\to0`$: the mutual part is $`-(\sigma_1\tau_1^2/M_1)\langle\partial_1M^{(2)}\rangle=-\tau_1^2\gamma_1\langle\partial_1\delta\rho\rangle`$, independent of $`\sigma_1`$ and $`k_1`$: the common local scale of 06-ilang-source at leading order.
3. **Equal parameters.** Two types with equal parameters, coupling, branch and widths, envelopes mirror-symmetric about the midpoint and without weight next to the cuts: $`\partial_2W=-\partial_1W`$ and the mirror maps one sum of (2.12) to minus the other, so $`a_1=-a_2`$, and (2.13) gives $`-\mu_2/\mu_1=-1`$.
