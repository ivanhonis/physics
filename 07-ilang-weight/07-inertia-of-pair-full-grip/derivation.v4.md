## Derivation

### Step 1. Held levels, half-gaps and odd parts

By S1, non-interacting objects in the lowest state all occupy the lowest one-object level, so the held level of $`\mathcal G`$ is $`E(j,l)=N_f\varepsilon_0(g_1\,\text{at}\,j,\,g_2\,\text{at}\,l)`$ and the mutual term $`M(r)`$, $`r=l-j`$, is (4.2) of 06-ilang-source/04-profile. Define, with the other coupling held fixed,
```math
\sigma_iR_i:=\tfrac{N_f}2\big[\varepsilon_0(g_i)-\varepsilon_0(-g_i)\big],\quad c_i^2:=\frac{\sigma_iR_i}{\mu_i},\quad M_i^{o}(r):=\tfrac12\big[M(g_i)-M(-g_i)\big](r),\quad M_i^e:=M-M_i^o,\quad \bar f(\rho):=\tfrac14\big[f(\rho-1)+2f(\rho)+f(\rho+1)\big].\qquad\text{(7.1)}
```
$`\sigma_iR_i`$ is the branch-odd part of the cost (5.3) ($`\theta=0`$). Rayleigh–Schrödinger perturbation of $`\varepsilon_0`$ in $`\sum_ag_a\delta_{x_a}`$, with $`\varepsilon_q-\varepsilon_0=2t_f(1-\cos q)`$ and $`\frac1L\sum_{q\ne0}\frac{e^{iqx}}{2(1-\cos q)}=\ell(x)`$, gives $`N_f\varepsilon_0=-2N_ft_f+n\sum_ag_a-\frac n{t_f}\sum_{a,b}g_ag_b\ell(x_a-x_b)+O(n\lvert g\rvert\eta^2)`$. Hence $`\sigma_iR_i=ng_i[1+O(\eta^2)]`$; $`M=B(r)[1+O(\eta)]`$ with $`B`$ of (4.2) of 04-weight-of-bond, and since $`B\propto g_1g_2`$ is odd in each coupling, $`M_1^o=M_2^o=B[1+O(\eta)]`$, $`M_i^e=O(\eta B)`$. In (5.3) the field state of the flipped branch differs at first order in $`g_i`$ by a vector of squared norm $`O(\eta^2)`$ per object, so $`c_i^2=\tau_i^2[1+O(N_f\eta^2)]`$ and $`\mu_i=ng_i\tau_i^{-2}[1+O(N_f\eta^2)]`$; in particular $`\mathrm{sign}\mu_i=\mathrm{sign}g_i`$.

### Step 2. Band contract and selected level (item 1)

*Group and distances.* Each motion term conserves $`x_i`$ plus the branch index mod 2, so $`r+s_1+s_2`$ mod 2 is conserved ($`s_i=0`$ on the selected, $`1`$ on the flipped branch). $`\mathcal G`$ ($`r`$ even, $`s_1=s_2=0`$) lies in the class with $`r+s_1+s_2`$ even, together with its field excitations, the single flips at odd $`r`$ and the joint flip at even $`r`$. The class with odd $`r`$ has its own selected level. Everything below holds for either class, except the narrow-pair asymptotics ($`w\lesssim1`$), which differ (end of this step). The motion terms connect $`\mathcal G`$ at first order only to the single flips; the joint flip is reached at second order. Its held levels are $`E_{--}(r')=N_f\varepsilon_0(-g_1\,\text{at}\,j,-g_2\,\text{at}\,l)`$, and by the expansion of Step 1, whose second-order terms are even under the joint flip, $`E(r)-E_{--}(r')=2n(g_1+g_2)+B(r)-B(r')+O(n\lvert g\rvert\eta^2)`$. $`B`$ ranges over an interval of length $`\lvert F\rvert L/4=\frac32\lvert B(0)\rvert[1+O(L^{-2})]`$ ($`V`$ below), so the two held spectra are at distance $`d_\times=2n\lvert g_1+g_2\rvert-\frac32\lvert B(0)\rvert`$ at leading order. The single flips are at $`2n\lvert g_i\rvert[1+O(\eta)]`$ ((5.4) for each body), and the excited field states of the flipped sectors at $`\varepsilon_1-2n\sum_{\rm flipped}g_i`$; this gives $`d`$ of S2.

Under S2 the derivation of (2.10) applies unchanged: it uses only that $`\mathcal G`$ is isolated in the held spectrum and that each motion term moves one body by one place and flips its branch; the full grip changes only the held levels ($`\theta=0`$ in (5.1)). So (2.10) holds with error $`O(\tau^4/d^3)`$; the terms through the joint flip are of fourth order, with denominators $`\ge d`$, and belong to it. Where (N×) fails, in particular for $`g_1=-g_2`$, where reflection maps the selected held spectrum onto the jointly flipped one, $`\mathcal G`$ is not isolated and the scalar band contract is not used; such descriptions are outside the class. With (2.1) the ansatz $`\psi(j,l)=e^{iPj}g(l-j)`$ gives (6.1) unchanged (the double step of $`b_1`$ carries the relative field coordinates: phase $`e^{-2iP}`$).

*Amplitudes.* The double step of $`b_i`$ across the relative bond $`\rho`$ passes through the held level with $`b_i`$ flipped at relative place $`\rho`$. Since $`M(-g_i)=-M_i^o+M_i^e`$, the gap from the held level at $`r=\rho\mp1`$ is
```math
\Delta_i(r;\rho)=2\sigma_iR_i+M_i^o(r)+M_i^o(\rho)+M_i^e(r)-M_i^e(\rho).\qquad\text{(7.2)}
```
Second-order Schrieffer–Wolff with symmetrized denominators gives $`\kappa_i(\rho)=-\frac{\tau_i^2a_i^2}8\big[\Delta_i(\rho-1;\rho)^{-1}+\Delta_i(\rho+1;\rho)^{-1}\big]`$, up to the excited-state term of (5.3); for $`M=0`$ this is $`\bar\kappa_i=-1/(8\mu_i)`$ of (5.3). The difference $`-1/(8\kappa_i(\rho))-\mu_i`$ vanishes with the partner's coupling $`g_j`$. Its first order is the shift of the denominators by $`M_i^o=O(\sigma_iR_i\eta_j)`$. The next terms are relative $`O(\eta)`$ to it: the second order of this expansion, relative $`O(\eta_j)`$, and the partner's change of the field overlaps, relative $`O(\eta_i)`$. The $`M_i^e`$ terms combine to a second difference, which is negligible. So the **local inertia** of $`b_i`$ is
```math
m_i(\rho):=-\frac1{8\kappa_i(\rho)}=\mu_i+\frac{\bar M_i^o(\rho)}{c_i^2}+O(\lvert\mu_i\rvert\eta\eta_j)=\mu_i+\frac{\bar B(\rho)}{\tau_i^2}\big[1+O(\eta)\big].\qquad\text{(7.3)}
```
The partner's mark changes each body's local inertia by the bond average of the mutual term over that body's own $`\tau_i^2`$. The same gaps give $`d_i(r)=-2\bar\kappa_i[1-\bar M_i^o(r)/\sigma_iR_i]`$. (6.5) and (6.6) hold: they use only the form (6.1), a real $`f_0`$ at $`P=0`$, and Hellmann–Feynman in the bond magnitudes and the flux.

*Bound level.* $`U(r)-U(0)=V(r)[1+O(\eta,\tau^2/d^2)]`$ with $`V(r):=B(r)-B(0)=F\,r(L-r)/L`$ on $`0\le r\le L`$. Near the edge $`P=0`$, $`k=0`$ of the relative band, (6.1) has relative hop $`\bar t_0=-\frac18(\mu_1^{-1}+\mu_2^{-1})`$: the selected level is the lowest if $`\bar t_0<0`$, the highest if $`\bar t_0>0`$. It is bound at $`r\approx0`$ iff the extremum of $`V`$ at $`r=0`$ pushes it out of the band: a minimum ($`F>0`$) for the lowest, a maximum ($`F<0`$) for the highest, i.e. $`g_1g_2(\mu_1^{-1}+\mu_2^{-1})>0`$. With Step 1, in the class (S2 with (N×)):
```math
\text{selected level bound}\iff\tau_1^2g_2+\tau_2^2g_1>0,\qquad g_i=\sigma_i\gamma_iM_i .\qquad\text{(7.4)}
```
Both $`g_i>0`$: bound, lowest. Both $`g_i<0`$: not bound. Opposite signs: bound (highest) iff the inequality holds. Otherwise the extreme level sits at $`r\approx L/2`$, where $`V`$ has its other extremum (control: case D of the code). In the continuum limit of (6.1), $`-4\lvert\bar t_0\rvert\partial_r^2+\lvert F\rvert\lvert r\rvert`$, the width is $`w`$ of S4; $`f_0>0`$ (Perron–Frobenius), so $`b_0>0`$ and $`0<\beta_0\le1`$. Write $`\bar B_0:=\sum_\rho b_0\bar B/\beta_0`$ for the bond-weighted mutual term.
- *Broad pairs* ($`w\gg1`$, either class): $`1-\beta_0=O(w^{-2})`$. The bond weights $`b_0/\beta_0`$ differ from $`f_0^2`$ by relative $`O(w^{-2})`$ in shape, and $`V`$ has slope $`\le\lvert F\rvert`$ with a kink at $`r=0`$, where $`f_0^2=O(1/w)`$. Hence $`\bar B_0-\langle M\rangle_0=O(\eta\lvert B(0)\rvert)+O(\lvert F\rvert/w)`$, and $`\langle V\rangle_0=O(\lvert F\rvert w)`$.
- *Narrow pairs, even class* ($`w\ll1`$): $`f_0`$ concentrates at $`r=0`$, $`f_0(\pm2)\simeq\lvert\bar t_0/V(2)\rvert`$, so $`\beta_0\simeq\frac{w^3}4\frac L{L-2}`$, $`\langle V\rangle_0=O(w^6\lvert F\rvert)`$, and $`\bar B_0\to\bar B(\pm1)=B(0)+F(1-\frac3{2L})`$.
- *Narrow pairs, odd class* ($`w\ll1`$): the sites $`r=\pm1`$ are degenerate and joined by the hop across $`\rho=0`$, so $`f_0(\pm1)\to2^{-1/2}`$, $`\beta_0\to\frac12`$, $`\langle V\rangle_0\to V(1)=F(1-\frac1L)`$, and $`\bar B_0\to\bar B(0)=B(0)+F\frac{L-1}{2L}`$.

So $`\langle V\rangle_0/\lvert B(0)\rvert=O(\max(w,1)/L)`$ in both classes, and $`\bar B_0-\langle M\rangle_0`$ is $`O(\lvert F\rvert)`$, not small against $`\lvert F\rvert`$, for narrow pairs.

### Step 3. Inertia of the pair (item 2)

By (7.3), $`m(\rho)=\mu_1+\mu_2+\delta m(\rho)`$, $`\delta m=\sum_i\bar M_i^o/c_i^2+e(\rho)`$, $`e=O(\sum_i\lvert\mu_i\rvert\eta\eta_j)`$. In (6.6), $`1/\sum_\rho b_0/m`$ is exact in the constant part of $`\delta m`$, which it divides by $`\beta_0`$: a constant $`e`$ shifts $`1/E_b''(0)`$ by $`e/\beta_0`$. The variation of $`\delta m`$ over the pair enters first, and at second order as $`-\mathrm{Var}(\delta m)/((\mu_1+\mu_2)\beta_0)`$ (variance in the weights $`b_0/\beta_0`$), relative $`O(\eta w^2/L^2)`$ to the first-order term. The band contract differs from the exact contract by relative $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ in the $`r`$-dependent part of the hops. In their constant part it differs as for each body alone, and the exact $`\mu_i`$ absorb this up to relative $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ of the lattice part. Hence
```math
D_0=D^{\rm lat}\Big[1+O\Big(\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\Big)\Big]+D^{\rm mark}\Big[1+O\Big(\eta,\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\Big)\Big]-\frac{\rho_L}{E''_{\rm line}}+O\Big(\frac{\rho_L^2}{E''_{\rm line}}\Big),\quad D^{\rm lat}=(\mu_1+\mu_2)\frac{1-\beta_0}{\beta_0},\quad D^{\rm mark}=\frac1{\beta_0^2}\sum_\rho b_0\sum_i\frac{\bar M_i^o}{c_i^2}=\Big(\frac1{\tau_1^2}+\frac1{\tau_2^2}\Big)\frac{\sum_\rho b_0\bar B}{\beta_0^2}\big[1+O(\eta)\big].\qquad\text{(7.5)}
```
Both parts carry the factor $`1/\beta_0`$: $`\sum_\rho b_0\bar B=\beta_0B(0)+\sum_\rho b_0\bar V`$ with $`\lvert\sum_\rho b_0\bar V\rvert/\beta_0=O(\lvert F\rvert\max(w,1))`$, so $`\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert=(\tau_1^{-2}+\tau_2^{-2})\lvert B(0)\rvert\beta_0^{-1}[1+O(\eta,\max(w,1)/L)]`$. Each remainder is relative to the part it corrects, so (7.5) holds for small $`\beta_0`$ as well. This is (6.8) with $`s_c=+1`$ and $`c_i=\tau_i`$, the full-grip speeds of (5.7), plus the flux remainder of (6.6). $`D^{\rm lat}`$ comes from the lattice form of the bands, $`D^{\rm mark}`$ from the mutual term. Each body's inertia counts the part of $`M`$ odd in its own coupling, and at leading order this is all of $`M`$ for each body.

*Flux remainder.* By (6.6), $`E_b''(0)=E''_{\rm line}(1+\rho_L)`$ with $`\rho_L:=\tilde\Phi'(0)^2\partial^2_{\tilde\Phi}\epsilon_0/E''_{\rm line}`$. This term comes from circuits of the relative motion around the ring, and $`\tilde\Phi'(0)^2=O(L^2)`$ outside the tuned case.
- For $`w\gg1`$ the continuum bound state (Step 2) decays as $`\exp[-\frac23(\lvert r\rvert/w)^{3/2}]`$, and a circuit passes $`r\approx\frac L2`$. So $`\lvert\rho_L\rvert=O(e^{-c(L/w)^{3/2}})`$ up to powers of $`L/w`$, with $`c>0`$.
- For $`w\lesssim1`$ in the even class the locator expansion about $`r=0`$ gives the lowest circuit. It has $`\frac L2`$ hops $`\simeq\bar t_0`$ and $`\frac L2-1`$ denominators $`\lvert V(2k)\rvert\ge\lvert F\rvert`$, so $`\partial^2_{\tilde\Phi}\epsilon_0=O(\lvert\bar t_0\rvert^{L/2}/\lvert F\rvert^{L/2-1})`$. With $`E''_{\rm line}=O(\bar t_0^2/\lvert F\rvert)`$ ($`b_0(\pm1)\simeq\lvert\bar t_0/V(2)\rvert`$), this gives $`\lvert\rho_L\rvert=O(L^2(w^3/4)^{L/2-2})`$. This is $`O(w^3)`$ at $`L=6`$ and not exponentially small (control F of the code). In the odd class at $`w\lesssim1`$ the state sits on $`r=\pm1`$ with $`\beta_0\to\frac12`$; $`\rho_L`$ is not estimated there and is kept explicit.

Against the mutual part of the inertia the flux term is $`\varepsilon_L:=\lvert\rho_L\rvert/(E''_{\rm line}\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert)`$. Since $`E''_{\rm line}=\beta_0(\mu_1+\mu_2)^{-1}[1+O(\eta)]`$ and $`\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert\ge2\eta_{\min}\lvert\mu_1+\mu_2\rvert\beta_0^{-1}[1+O(\eta,\max(w,1)/L)]`$ (from $`\lvert g_1g_2\rvert(\tau_1^{-2}+\tau_2^{-2})\ge\min_i\lvert g_i\rvert\sum_i\lvert g_i\rvert\tau_i^{-2}`$), we get $`\varepsilon_L\le\lvert\rho_L\rvert/(2\eta_{\min})[1+O(\eta,\max(w,1)/L)]`$. At small $`L`$ these corrections are not small (control F at $`L=6`$: $`\varepsilon_L=1.9`$). Wherever $`w`$ may be small, $`\rho_L`$ and $`\varepsilon_L`$ are kept explicit.

### Step 4. Cost of the bond and inertia against cost (item 3)

With $`f_0`$ real, $`E_b(0)=\sum_rf_0^2U+2\sum_\rho b_0\,t(\rho;0)`$, $`E_i(0)=E_i^{\rm held}+\bar d_i+2\bar\kappa_i`$, $`E^{\rm held}_{\rm pair}(r)=E_1^{\rm held}+E_2^{\rm held}-E_\varnothing+M(r)`$ (definition (4.2)). The partner shifts $`d_i`$ and $`\kappa_i`$ by the relative amount $`-\bar M_i^o/\sigma_iR_i`$ (Step 2), and $`\bar d_i=-2\bar\kappa_i`$, so these shifts cancel up to the factor $`1-\beta_0`$:
```math
\Delta E_0=\langle M\rangle_0+K_0\big[1+O(\eta)\big],\qquad K_0=2\bar t_0(\beta_0-1)=\tfrac14(\mu_1^{-1}+\mu_2^{-1})(1-\beta_0).\qquad\text{(7.6)}
```
**(i)** Since $`(\mu_1+\mu_2)/(\mu_1^{-1}+\mu_2^{-1})=\mu_1\mu_2`$, $`D^{\rm lat}=4\mu_1\mu_2K_0/\beta_0`$ exactly, and by (7.5) $`D^{\rm mark}=(\tau_i^{-2}+\tau_j^{-2})\bar B_0/\beta_0\,[1+O(\eta)]`$ with $`\bar B_0`$ of Step 2. With $`v=\tau_i`$, the speed of (5.7) of either body, and $`j\neq i`$:
```math
D_0-\frac{\Delta E_0}{\tau_i^2}=\frac{4\mu_1\mu_2}{\beta_0}K_0\Big[1-\frac{\beta_0}{4\mu_1\mu_2\tau_i^2}\Big]+\frac{\bar B_0}{\beta_0\tau_j^2}+\frac1{\tau_i^2}\Big[\frac{\bar B_0}{\beta_0}-\langle M\rangle_0\Big]-\frac{\rho_L}{E''_{\rm line}}+O\Big(\Big(\eta+\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\Big)\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert+\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\lvert D^{\rm lat}\rvert+\frac{\rho_L^2}{E''_{\rm line}}\Big),\qquad\lvert4\mu_1\mu_2\tau_i^2\rvert=\frac{(2n\lvert g_1\rvert)(2n\lvert g_2\rvert)}{\tau_j^2}\ge\frac{d^2}{\tau_j^2}\gg1.\qquad\text{(7.7)}
```
This holds for every $`w`$ of S4 and both classes; $`\bar B_0`$ is not replaced by $`\langle M\rangle_0`$, since they differ by $`O(\lvert F\rvert)`$ for narrow pairs (Step 2). The lattice part exceeds $`K_0/v^2`$ by a factor $`\ge d^2/\tau_j^2`$. The mark part exceeds $`\langle M\rangle_0/v^2`$ by the two middle terms; the first of them is $`\frac{\tau_i^2}{\tau_i^2+\tau_j^2}D^{\rm mark}`$. For $`g_1g_2<0`$ the lattice term and $`\bar B_0/(\beta_0\tau_j^2)`$ are positive ($`\mu_1\mu_2K_0>0`$; $`\bar B_0>0`$, as $`B(0)\simeq\lvert F\rvert L/6`$ and $`\lvert\bar B_0-B(0)\rvert=O(\lvert F\rvert\max(w,1))`$ by S4). The last term is $`[\langle M\rangle_0(1-\beta_0)+\bar B_0-\langle M\rangle_0]/\beta_0`$; by Step 2 it is positive for $`w\ll1`$ (even class: $`\beta_0\to0`$; odd class: $`\bar B_0-\langle M\rangle_0\to\lvert F\rvert\frac{L-1}{2L}`$) and for $`1\ll w\ll L`$ ($`\langle M\rangle_0(1-\beta_0)\sim\lvert F\rvert L/w^2`$ against $`\lvert F\rvert/w`$). There (i) fails unless the remainder and the flux term cancel the displayed terms, which needs $`\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2=O(\eta+\tau^2/d^2)`$ and the other two terms small against $`\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert`$: the limit of (7.8). For $`g_1,g_2>0`$ the lattice and mark terms have opposite signs, and (i) holds only on the surface where they cancel, a tuned relation between $`1-\beta_0`$ and $`\eta`$. Elsewhere, no.

**(ii)** In S5, by (7.5), $`\lvert D^{\rm lat}\rvert=\delta_m\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert[1+O(\eta,w/L)]`$: the $`\beta_0`$ factors cancel, and the mutual term dominates the inertia. With the tuned case excluded, $`\lvert\mu_1+\mu_2\rvert=\lvert\mu_1^{-1}+\mu_2^{-1}\rvert\lvert\mu_1\mu_2\rvert\ge c\max_i\lvert\mu_i\rvert\ge\frac c2\sum_i\lvert\mu_i\rvert`$. With $`\lvert\mu_i\rvert=n\lvert g_i\rvert\tau_i^{-2}`$ and $`\lvert B(0)\rvert=2n\min_i\lvert g_i\rvert\,\eta`$ this gives $`1-\beta_0\le4\eta\delta_m/c\ll\eta`$; so $`w\gg1`$ and $`\beta_0=1-O(\eta\delta_m)`$. The converse fails: for $`\tau_1=\tau_2`$, $`0<g_2\ll g_1`$, $`\delta_m\simeq(1-\beta_0)/(4\eta_2)`$, which needs $`1-\beta_0\ll\eta_2`$. By (7.7), $`\lvert K_0\rvert=\beta_0\lvert D^{\rm lat}\rvert/\lvert4\mu_1\mu_2\rvert\le2\delta_m(\tau_{\max}^2/d^2)\lvert B(0)\rvert[1+O(\eta)]`$, so $`\Delta E_0=\langle M\rangle_0[1+O(\delta_m\tau^2/d^2)]`$. For $`w\gg1`$, Step 2 gives $`\bar B_0=\langle M\rangle_0+O(\eta\lvert B(0)\rvert)+O(\lvert F\rvert/w)`$. As $`\lvert F\rvert/\lvert B(0)\rvert=O(1/L)`$ and $`w^{-2}=O(1-\beta_0)=O(\eta\delta_m)`$, this is $`\bar B_0=\langle M\rangle_0[1+O(\eta,\delta_m)]`$; only here is $`\bar B_0`$ replaced by $`\langle M\rangle_0`$. Against the mutual scale $`\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert`$ the omitted terms are then: the lattice part, $`O(\delta_m)`$; its remainder, $`O(\delta_m\tau^2/d^2)`$; the mark remainder, $`O(\eta,\tau^2/d^2)`$; the ring remainder, $`\varepsilon_L`$, which is $`O(e^{-c(L/w)^{3/2}}/\eta_{\min})`$ since S5 implies $`w\gg1`$; $`1/\beta_0-1=O(\eta\delta_m)`$; and $`K_0`$ in $`\Delta E_0`$, $`O(\delta_m\tau^2/d^2)`$. Hence
```math
D_0=\Big(\frac1{\tau_1^2}+\frac1{\tau_2^2}\Big)\Delta E_0\Big[1+O\Big(\eta,\delta_m,\frac{\tau^2}{d^2},\varepsilon_L\Big)\Big];\qquad\text{equal speeds }\tau_1=\tau_2=v:\ D_0=\frac{2\Delta E_0}{v^2}.\qquad\text{(7.8)}
```
The binding cost has inertia, but $`(\tau_1^{-2}+\tau_2^{-2})\Delta E_0\ne\Delta E_0/\tau_i^2`$: the mutual term is odd in both couplings, so it enters both half-gaps (7.3) but the cost only once. It approaches $`\Delta E_0/\tau_i^2`$ only for $`\tau_i/\tau_j\to0`$, with relative deviation $`\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2`$.

### Step 5. Weight and fall (item 4)

*Fixed source and band contract.* The source $`g_s\Phi_{x_s}`$ of S6 breaks (2.1), so it has no $`P`$ sectors. It is a held object, so S2 and the derivation of (2.10) apply with the source among the held objects: the band contract in laboratory coordinates is $`H_0+g_sH_1`$, with $`H_0`$ of (2.10) and $`H_1`$ its first derivative in $`g_s`$. $`H_1`$ is the held weight $`\partial_{g_s}E(j,l)`$ (diagonal) plus the change of $`K_1,K_2`$, the kinetic part. The source changes each gap by the relative amount $`O(\phi)`$, as the partner does in (7.3). So the $`O(\tau^4/d^3)`$ error of (2.10) has a gradient $`O(\tau^4\lvert\phi'\rvert/d^3)`$, relative $`O(\tau^4/(d^4\eta_{\min}\kappa_s))`$ to $`W_b'`$ of (7.10) below ($`\lvert W_b'\rvert\simeq2\kappa_s\lvert B(0)\phi'\rvert`$, $`\lvert B(0)\rvert\ge d\,\eta_{\min}`$). The fall in the fixed source is derived in this band contract.

*Internal state.* Use the midpoint $`X=j+h/2`$ and the representative $`h\in(-\frac L2,\frac L2]`$ of $`r`$; the formulas are written for the even class (in the odd class $`\Theta_P`$ starts at the bond $`\rho=0`$). The eigenvectors of the selected level of $`H_0`$ are $`\Psi_P=L^{-1/2}e^{iPX}u_P(h)`$, $`P\in\frac{2\pi}L\mathbb Z_L`$, where $`u_P`$ is the selected eigenvector of (6.1) in this gauge. Its hop from $`h+2`$ to $`h`$ is $`e^{iP}t(\rho;P)=\kappa_1(\rho)e^{-iP}+\kappa_2(\rho)e^{iP}=t(\rho;0)[\cos P-i\nu(\rho)\sin P]`$, $`\rho=h+1`$. On the line (the chart of $`h`$ cut at $`h\approx\frac L2`$) a gauge transformation makes the hops real:
```math
u_P(h)=e^{i\Theta_P(h)}v_P(h),\qquad\Theta_P(h)=\sum_{\rho\in(0,h)}\theta(\rho;P),\qquad\theta=\arctan[\nu(\rho)\tan P],\qquad\nu(\rho):=\frac{m_2(\rho)-m_1(\rho)}{m_1(\rho)+m_2(\rho)},\qquad w_P=w\Big[1-\frac{1-\bar\nu^2}6P^2\Big(1+O\Big(\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\Big)\Big)+O(P^4)\Big].\qquad\text{(7.16)}
```
The sum runs over the bonds between $`0`$ and $`h`$, with the sign of $`h`$, so $`\Theta_P`$ is odd in $`h`$. $`v_P`$ is the real positive selected state with hops $`t(\rho;0)[1-(1-\nu(\rho)^2)\sin^2P]^{1/2}`$; it is even in $`h`$, as $`U`$ and the $`\kappa_i`$ are. On the ring the phase must close at $`h\approx\frac L2`$ (its total is the flux $`\tilde\Phi`$ of Step 3), where $`v_P=O(e^{-c(L/w)^{3/2}})`$ for $`w\gg1`$; this is the error of the line. By (7.3), $`\nu(\rho)=\bar\nu+O(\eta\lvert\rho\rvert/L)`$ with $`\bar\nu:=\nu(\pm1)=\frac{\mu_2-\mu_1}{\mu_1+\mu_2}+O(\eta)`$, and $`\lvert\bar\nu\rvert\le2/c+O(\eta)`$ outside the tuned case. For $`w\gg1`$ the continuum limit (Step 2) has a width $`\propto\lvert\text{hop}\rvert^{1/3}`$; this gives the width $`w_P`$ of $`v_P`$. The $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ comes from the $`r`$-dependence of the hops, which adds to $`V`$ a part of relative size $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ that scales with the hop.

*Centre contract.* By (7.16), $`\Psi_P=L^{-1/2}e^{iPY}v_P(h)`$ up to phases $`O(P^3h,\eta Ph^2/L)`$, with the centre $`Y:=X+\frac12\bar\nu h=\frac{m_1x_1+m_2x_2}{m_1+m_2}`$ ($`m_i:=m_i(\pm1)`$). The selected band is separated from the other levels of the relative motion by $`\Delta_{\rm rel}`$ ($`\simeq1.32\lvert F\rvert w`$ for $`w\gg1`$: $`\lvert a_1\rvert-\lvert a_1'\rvert`$ of the Airy function, the lowest odd and even levels of $`-\partial_\xi^2+\lvert\xi\rvert`$). At first order in $`g_s`$, projection on the band gives the centre contract
```math
h_c=E_b(\hat P)+V,\qquad V_{PP'}=g_s\langle\Psi_P|H_1|\Psi_{P'}\rangle.\qquad\text{(7.14)}
```
The other levels enter at $`O(g_s^2/\Delta_{\rm rel})`$. The interband parts of $`H_1`$ and of $`\hat X`$ give bounded oscillations of frequency $`\ge\Delta_{\rm rel}`$ and amplitude $`O(g_s)`$; they do not accumulate. The projected midpoint is $`\Pi\hat X\Pi=i\partial_P+A`$ with $`A=i\langle u_P|\partial_Pu_P\rangle=-\sum_hv_P^2\,\partial_P\Theta_P=0`$, as $`v_P^2`$ is even and $`\Theta_P`$ odd. So it is the centre $`\hat Y`$ conjugate to $`P`$, and $`\Pi\hat h\Pi=0`$. The band transformation adds to $`\hat X`$ an internal operator of size $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$; it commutes with $`E_b(\hat P)`$ and changes the acceleration below by a relative $`O(\tau^2/(d^2w_X^2))`$. Let $`K:=\frac{P+P'}2`$, $`q:=P-P'`$; then $`\Theta_P-\Theta_{P'}=\frac q2\nu_Kh+O(q^3h)`$ with $`\nu_K:=\partial_K\theta=\bar\nu[1+(1-\bar\nu^2)K^2+O(K^4)]`$. Hence $`V`$ is the Weyl operator in $`(\hat Y,\hat P)`$ of the symbol
```math
\mathcal W(Y,K)=\sum_hv_K(h)^2\,H_1\big(Y-\tfrac12\nu_Kh,\;h\big),\qquad\text{(7.17)}
```
up to terms with $`\partial_Y^2H_1`$ times $`\partial_q^2(v_Pv_{P'})`$, which are constant in $`Y`$ at first order in the couplings. The kinetic part of $`H_1`$, a hop, enters at the centre of the hop. (7.17) makes the kernel of $`V`$ explicit: for each relative place $`h`$ the source is sampled at the midpoint displaced by $`\frac12\nu_Kh`$. Its first moment is $`-\frac12\bar\nu h`$ per relative place, $`O(\lvert\bar\nu\rvert w)`$, and zero in total ($`v_K^2`$ is even). Its second moment is $`\frac14\bar\nu^2\langle h^2\rangle_0=O(\bar\nu^2w^2)`$, so its range is $`O(\lvert\bar\nu\rvert w)`$. $`\mathcal W`$ is even in $`K`$: $`\mathcal W=\mathcal W_0(Y)+K^2\mathcal W_2(Y)+O(K^4)`$. The row sum $`W_{\rm ex}`$ of $`V`$ (its $`P=0`$ symbol in the gauge $`\langle u_P|u_0\rangle>0`$, which is that of (7.16)) has $`W_{\rm ex}'=g_s\mathcal W_0'`$ up to $`\partial_Y^3\mathcal W_2`$, which vanishes at first order in the couplings.

*Acceleration from rest.* For the symbol $`E_b(K)+g_s\mathcal W`$ of $`h_c`$, Ehrenfest's theorem with the Moyal bracket gives, at first order in $`g_s`$, $`\frac{d^2}{d\lambda^2}\langle\hat Y\rangle=g_s\langle-E_b''(K)\,\partial_Y\mathcal W+E_b'(K)\,\partial_Y\partial_K\mathcal W\rangle`$, up to $`\partial_Y^3\mathcal W`$ times odd derivatives of $`E_b`$, whose average vanishes for the start below. Take a start at rest in the selected band, with real envelope of width $`w_X`$ in $`Y`$, centred at $`\bar X`$ (the mean projected midpoint). Its Wigner function is even in $`K`$, with $`\langle K^2\rangle=1/(4w_X^2)`$. Then
$`\ddot{\bar X}=-g_sE_b''(0)\langle\mathcal W_0'\rangle-g_s\langle K^2\rangle\big[\tfrac12E_b''''(0)\langle\mathcal W_0'\rangle-E_b''(0)\langle\mathcal W_2'\rangle\big]+O(w_X^{-4})`$.
- The bracket is relative $`O(w_X^{-2})`$. $`E_b''''/E_b''=O(1)`$ in the class, since the band depends on $`P`$ through the hop magnitude of (7.16). The kinetic part of $`\mathcal W_2`$ is the source's change of the inertias by the relative amount $`-\phi`$ ((7.3) with the source in place of the partner), $`g_s\mathcal W_2\simeq-\frac12E_b''\phi`$; it is relative $`O(E_b''/(n\lvert g_1+g_2\rvert))=O(\tau^2/d^2)`$. The $`v_K`$ part changes $`\langle V\rangle_0`$ by $`-\frac16(1-\bar\nu^2)K^2\langle V\rangle_0`$.
- Over the start, $`\langle\mathcal W_0'\rangle=\mathcal W_0'(\bar X)+\frac{w_X^2}2\mathcal W_0'''(\bar X)+\dots`$. At first order in the couplings $`\mathcal W_0`$ is quadratic away from $`x_s`$ ($`\ell''=\frac1L`$). So $`\mathcal W_0'''`$ comes from the second-order terms of (7.9), (7.10), through $`\kappa_s\simeq(1-2u)^2`$ and $`\phi'\propto1-2u`$: $`W_i'''/W_i'\simeq-48g_i\ell_0/(t_fL^2)`$ and $`W_b'''/W_b'\simeq24/(L^2\kappa_s)`$. The tail of the start at the source gives $`O(e^{-s^2/(2w_X^2)})`$.

*Fall of the pair.* Let $`W_0(Y):=g_s\sum_hv_0(h)^2H_1(Y,h)`$ be the midpoint-conditioned weight of the $`P=0`$ state, kinetic part included. In the exact contract it is $`g_s\rho_0(x_s-Y)`$, with $`\rho_0`$ the field density at distance $`x_s-Y`$ from the midpoint in the $`P=0`$ eigenvector. By (7.17), $`g_s\mathcal W_0-W_0=g_s\sum_hv_0^2[H_1(Y-\frac12\bar\nu h,h)-H_1(Y,h)]`$; the variation of $`\nu(\rho)`$ adds a displacement odd in $`h`$, which drops out in the same way. Expand in $`\bar\nu h`$. The first-order terms in the couplings, $`n[g_1\phi(x_1)+g_2\phi(x_2)]/g_s`$ and the kinetic part, have $`\partial_Y^2H_1`$ even in $`h`$ and $`\partial_Y^3H_1=0`$, so they give nothing. In the second-order terms of (4.5), (4.7) use $`Q:=2\ell_0\ell+\ell^2-\frac2L\ell^{(2)}`$, $`Q'''=\frac4L\ell'`$, $`(\phi(x_1)\phi(x_2))'''\simeq6\phi''\phi'`$ and $`(\ell^{(2)})'''=\ell'`$. At first order in $`\bar\nu`$ the self terms give $`-\bar\nu\langle h^2\rangle_0(g_2^2-g_1^2)\frac{ng_s\ell'}{t_f^2L}`$; the bond terms start at $`\bar\nu^2`$. Compare with $`\lvert W_b'\rvert\simeq\frac{L\kappa_s^{3/2}}3\frac{n\lvert g_sg_1g_2\rvert}{t_f^2}`$, use $`L\kappa_s^{1/2}>1`$, and use $`\lvert W_b'\rvert\ge2\eta_{\min}\kappa_s\lvert W_0'\rvert`$ at leading order ((7.9), (7.10)). Then the acceleration of the projected midpoint from rest is
```math
\ddot{\bar X}=-E_b''(0)\big[W_0'(\bar X)+\epsilon\,\lvert W_b'\rvert\big],\qquad\epsilon=O\Big(\frac1{w_X^2\eta_{\min}\kappa_s},\ \frac\eta{\eta_{\min}}\frac{w_X^2}{L^2\kappa_s},\ \lvert\bar\nu\rvert(1+\lvert\bar\nu\rvert)\frac{g_1^2+g_2^2}{\lvert g_1g_2\rvert}\frac{w^2}{L^2\kappa_s}\Big),\qquad\text{(7.15)}
```
for $`w\gg1`$, $`s\gg w`$ and $`w_X\ll s`$, up to $`O(e^{-c(L/w)^{3/2}},e^{-c(s/w)^{3/2}},e^{-s^2/(2w_X^2)})`$ and the $`O(\tau^4/(d^4\eta_{\min}\kappa_s))`$ of the band contract. The first two entries of $`\epsilon`$ come from the start (momentum spread, and variation of the gradient over the start), the third from the displacement of the centre from the midpoint; for equal bodies $`\bar\nu=0`$. By (7.11) below, $`E_b''(0)W_b'`$ is the bond's part of the acceleration. So $`\epsilon\ll1`$ puts every error below the mutual-order effect. This is the start-width hierarchy $`(\eta_{\min}\kappa_s)^{-1/2}\ll w_X\ll\min\big(s,\,L\kappa_s^{1/2}(\eta_{\min}/\eta)^{1/2}\big)`$, together with $`\lvert\bar\nu\rvert(1+\lvert\bar\nu\rvert)(g_1^2+g_2^2)w^2\ll\lvert g_1g_2\rvert L^2\kappa_s`$. A body alone has no internal coordinate, and (5.14) applies directly.

*Content of $`W_0`$.* $`W_0`$ contains the held densities and, through the kinetic part of $`H_1`$, the change of the hops: $`W_0'=\sum_hf_0^2(\partial_1+\partial_2)W_{\rm pair}`$ plus the kinetic part ($`v_0=f_0`$). By (7.3) with the source in place of the partner, $`\kappa_i,d_i`$ change by the relative amount $`-\phi`$ ($`M^o_{is}=ng_i\phi`$). So the kinetic energies weigh $`-(\text{kinetic energy})\,\phi'`$. A body alone at $`P=0`$ has none ($`\bar d_i+2\bar\kappa_i=0`$), and the bond part is $`-K_0\phi'`$. All such terms are relative $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ against $`ng\phi'`$. The code computes $`W_0`$ from the exact density $`\rho_0`$ (for odd $`h`$, the two places at distance $`k\mp\frac12`$ averaged), and cross-checks it with the Hellmann–Feynman shift under the operator $`g_s\sum_{j,l}\lvert j,l\rangle\langle j,l\rvert\otimes\Phi_{X(j,l)+s}`$. This operator is an auxiliary mathematical probe on the sector space, not a contract of the description.

*Lattice identities.* For $`1\le x\le L-1`$: $`\ell'(x)=-\frac12(1-\frac{2x}L)`$; $`(\ell^2)'=(2\ell+\frac1L)\ell'`$ (as $`\Lambda\ell=-\frac1L`$ there); and since $`\Lambda\ell^{(2)}=\ell`$, $`\ell^{(2)}`$ is even about $`0`$ and $`L/2`$, the cubic $`-\frac{Lx}{12}+\frac{x^2}4-\frac{x^3}{6L}=\frac{x(L-x)}{6}\ell'(x)`$ is $`(\ell^{(2)})'`$. Inserting these into the derivative of $`2\ell_0\ell+\ell^2-\frac2L\ell^{(2)}`$ in (4.5) gives $`4\ell_0\kappa_s\ell'`$ with $`\kappa_s`$ below, and $`\phi'=-\frac{2g_s}{t_f}\ell'`$:
```math
W_i'(X)=\phi'\big[ng_i+2\kappa_sC_i^{(2)}\big],\qquad C_i^{(2)}:=-\frac{ng_i^2\ell_0}{t_f},\qquad\kappa_s:=\frac{L^2(1-2u)^2+2}{L^2-1}=(1-2u)^2+O(L^{-2}),\qquad\text{(7.9)}
```
up to relative $`O(\eta^2)`$. $`C_i^{(2)}`$ is the second-order even part of the cost; it is weighed with $`2\kappa_s`$ but has no inertia by (5.3).

*Bond.* In (4.7) at $`x_{1,2}=X\mp r/2`$: $`f_0`$ is even in $`r`$ at $`P=0`$ (as $`V`$ and $`\kappa_i`$ are), so $`\langle r\rangle_0=0`$. As $`\phi`$ is quadratic away from $`x_s`$, $`\partial_X[W_b(X;r)-W_b(X;0)]=V(r)\phi'(X)+O(\lvert F\phi'\rvert r^2/L)`$ for $`\lvert r\rvert<s`$, and $`W_b(X;0)`$ is (4.5) with $`g_i^2\to2g_1g_2`$. So, with the kinetic part,
```math
W_b':=W_0'-W_1'-W_2'=\phi'\big[2\kappa_sB(0)+\langle V\rangle_0-K_0\big]+O\big(\eta\lvert B(0)\phi'\rvert\big)+O\big(\lvert F\phi'\rvert w^2/L\big).\qquad\text{(7.10)}
```
The first remainder is the next order of the bond weight in the couplings. Like $`W_b'`$ it is odd about the antipode, hence proportional to $`\phi'`$. $`\langle V\rangle_0=O(\lvert B(0)\rvert\max(w,1)/L)`$ (Step 2) and $`K_0`$ are kept; (7.10) needs no relation between $`\kappa_s`$ and the small parameters.

*Fall factors.* $`\Gamma_i:=E_i''(0)W_i'=W_i'/\mu_i`$ and $`\Gamma_{\rm pair}:=E_b''(0)W_0'`$ are the fall factors: by (5.14) for each body alone and by (7.15) for the pair, the acceleration from rest in the fixed source is $`-\Gamma`$, for the pair up to $`\epsilon\lvert E_b''W_b'\rvert`$ of (7.15). With $`\Gamma_b:=W_b'/D_0`$ and $`1/E_b''=\mu_1+\mu_2+D_0`$, exactly,
```math
\Gamma_{\rm pair}=\frac{\mu_1\Gamma_1+\mu_2\Gamma_2+D_0\Gamma_b}{\mu_1+\mu_2+D_0},\qquad\Gamma_i=\tau_i^2\phi'\Big[1-\frac{2\kappa_sg_i\ell_0}{t_f}+O\Big(\eta^2,N_f\eta^2,\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\Big)\Big].\qquad\text{(7.11)}
```
*Leading order.* $`W_b'`$ and $`D^{\rm mark}`$ are $`O(\eta)`$; the lattice inertia has weight $`-K_0\phi'=-\beta_0D^{\rm lat}\phi'/(4\mu_1\mu_2)`$, which is $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ against $`\tau^2\phi'D^{\rm lat}`$. Hence, with $`E_b''=E''_{\rm line}(1+\rho_L)`$ (Step 3), $`\Gamma_{\rm pair}=(1+\rho_L)\beta_0\bar\Gamma[1+O(\eta,\tau^2/d^2)]`$ for every $`w`$ of S4, with $`\bar\Gamma:=\frac{\mu_1\Gamma_1+\mu_2\Gamma_2}{\mu_1+\mu_2}=\frac{\mu_1\tau_1^2+\mu_2\tau_2^2}{\mu_1+\mu_2}\phi'[1+O(\eta)]`$. So $`\Gamma_{\rm pair}=\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2`$ at leading order (with $`1-\beta_0\to0`$, hence $`w\gg1`$ and $`\rho_L\to0`$) iff $`\tau_1=\tau_2`$. At $`w\lesssim1`$ and small $`L`$, the factor $`1+\rho_L`$ is not close to 1 (control F). For $`\tau_1\ne\tau_2`$ the bodies already fall differently; $`1-\beta_0`$ lowers the pair's fall, since the internal kinetic energy has inertia but no weight at this order.

*Order of the mutual term (S5, S6).* By (7.10), (7.8), with $`\langle V\rangle_0/(2\kappa_sB(0))=O(w/(L\kappa_s))`$, $`K_0/(\kappa_sB(0))=O(\delta_m\tau^2/(\kappa_sd^2))`$ (Step 4) and $`\Delta E_0=B(0)[1+O(\eta,w/L)]`$:
```math
\Gamma_b=\frac{2\kappa_s\,\phi'}{\tau_1^{-2}+\tau_2^{-2}}\Big[1+O\Big(\frac\eta{\kappa_s},\frac w{L\kappa_s},\delta_m,\frac{\tau^2}{d^2},\frac{\delta_m\tau^2}{\kappa_sd^2},\frac{\tau^4}{d^4\eta_{\min}\kappa_s},\varepsilon_L\Big)\Big],\qquad \Gamma_{\rm pair}-\bar\Gamma\overset{\tau_1=\tau_2=\tau}{=}4(1-\kappa_s)\frac{g_1g_2\ell_0}{t_f(g_1+g_2)}\tau^2\phi'.\qquad\text{(7.12)}
```
The sixth remainder is the error of the band contract with the source (Step 5). The fall of a start follows $`\Gamma_b`$ up to the further relative $`\epsilon`$ of (7.15). Under the hierarchy of S6, and with $`\varepsilon_L\ll1`$ (S5 gives $`\varepsilon_L=O(e^{-c(L/w)^{3/2}}/\eta_{\min})`$), every remainder is small. For equal speeds the bond's weight and inertia both carry the factor 2 of (7.8). The weight also carries $`\kappa_s=1-\frac{4s(L-s)-3}{L^2-1}<1`$ for $`1\le s\le L/2`$, so $`\Gamma_b=\kappa_s\tau^2\phi'`$ against $`\Gamma_i=\tau^2\phi'`$ at leading order. At leading order the binding cost is weighed in the same proportion as its inertia only in the limit $`u\to0`$ ($`w\ll s\ll L`$), where $`\Gamma_b/\Gamma_i=1-4u+O(u^2)`$ up to the remainders of (7.11), (7.12). At finite $`u`$, where $`1-\kappa_s\simeq4u`$ exceeds these remainders, it is weighed less. The second equation of (7.12) is the first order of the difference, from (7.11) with $`D_0\simeq2B(0)/\tau^2`$, $`\Gamma_b-\bar\Gamma\simeq(\kappa_s-1)\tau^2\phi'`$, $`\mu_1+\mu_2\simeq n(g_1+g_2)/\tau^2`$. Besides this, $`\bar\Gamma-\Gamma_i=O(\eta)`$ for $`g_1\ne g_2`$ from the self terms of (7.11).

*One body and both bodies.* By (7.11), (7.12), $`\Gamma_b/\Gamma_i=2\kappa_s/(1+\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2)`$ at leading order. Equality with both bodies at once ($`\Gamma_b=\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2`$, one ratio of weight to inertia for bond and bodies) needs $`\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2`$, i.e. $`\tau_1=\tau_2`$ at leading order, and then $`\kappa_s=1`$, i.e. $`u\to0`$. Equality with body $`i`$ alone holds at $`\kappa_s=\frac12(1+\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2)`$. This value lies in $`(\frac12,1)`$, and is reached at one source distance, $`u\simeq\frac12(1-\sqrt{\kappa_s})`$, iff $`\tau_i<\tau_j`$ (inside S6 if $`1-\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2\gg w/L`$); for $`\tau_i\ge\tau_j`$ it is not reached at any $`u>0`$. There $`\Gamma_b/\Gamma_j=\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2<1`$: the bond shares the proportion of the slower body only, at a tuned source position.

### Step 6. The hopping of the field (item 5)

At fixed $`n,\gamma_i,M_i,\tau_i`$ (so fixed $`g_i`$, $`\mu_i`$) and leading order: $`B,\langle M\rangle_0,D^{\rm mark},W_b'/\phi'\propto t_f^{-1}`$; $`F\propto t_f^{-1}`$, so $`w\propto t_f^{1/3}`$, and for $`w\gg1`$, $`1-\beta_0\propto t_f^{-2/3}`$, $`K_0,\langle V\rangle_0,D^{\rm lat}\propto t_f^{-2/3}`$; $`\phi'\propto g_s/t_f`$, $`\Gamma_i/\phi'`$ and $`\mu_i`$ are independent of $`t_f`$. Hence
```math
\frac{D^{\rm lat}}{\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert}\propto t_f^{1/3},\qquad \frac{D^{\rm mark}}{\Delta E_0}\to\frac1{\tau_1^2}+\frac1{\tau_2^2},\quad \frac{\Gamma_b}{\Gamma_i}\to\frac{2\kappa_s}{1+\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2}\quad\text{independent of }t_f.\qquad\text{(7.13)}
```
A stiffer field weakens the bond ($`\propto t_f^{-1}`$) and widens it, and S5 lies at the small-$`t_f`$ end, bounded by $`\eta\propto t_f^{-1}\ll1`$ and by $`\varepsilon_1\propto t_f`$ in S2. No value of $`t_f`$ restores the proportions of (7.8) and (7.12).

## Consistency checks

1. **Dimensions.** $`g,\tau,t_f,M,K_0`$ are energies (rates in $`\lambda`$); $`P,r,u,\phi,\kappa_s,\delta_m`$ are dimensionless.
