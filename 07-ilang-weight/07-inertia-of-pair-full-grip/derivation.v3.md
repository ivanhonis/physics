## Derivation

### Step 1. Held levels, half-gaps and odd parts

By S1, non-interacting objects in the lowest state all occupy the lowest one-object level, so the held level of $`\mathcal G`$ is $`E(j,l)=N_f\varepsilon_0(g_1\,\text{at}\,j,\,g_2\,\text{at}\,l)`$ and the mutual term $`M(r)`$, $`r=l-j`$, is (4.2) of 06-ilang-source/04-profile. Define, with the other coupling held fixed,
```math
\sigma_iR_i:=\tfrac{N_f}2\big[\varepsilon_0(g_i)-\varepsilon_0(-g_i)\big],\quad c_i^2:=\frac{\sigma_iR_i}{\mu_i},\quad M_i^{o}(r):=\tfrac12\big[M(g_i)-M(-g_i)\big](r),\quad M_i^e:=M-M_i^o,\quad \bar f(\rho):=\tfrac14\big[f(\rho-1)+2f(\rho)+f(\rho+1)\big].\qquad\text{(7.1)}
```
$`\sigma_iR_i`$ is the branch-odd part of the cost (5.3) ($`\theta=0`$). Rayleigh–Schrödinger perturbation of $`\varepsilon_0`$ in $`\sum_ag_a\delta_{x_a}`$, with $`\varepsilon_q-\varepsilon_0=2t_f(1-\cos q)`$ and $`\frac1L\sum_{q\ne0}\frac{e^{iqx}}{2(1-\cos q)}=\ell(x)`$, gives $`N_f\varepsilon_0=-2N_ft_f+n\sum_ag_a-\frac n{t_f}\sum_{a,b}g_ag_b\ell(x_a-x_b)+O(n\lvert g\rvert\eta^2)`$. Hence $`\sigma_iR_i=ng_i[1+O(\eta^2)]`$; $`M=B(r)[1+O(\eta)]`$ with $`B`$ of (4.2) of 04-weight-of-bond, and since $`B\propto g_1g_2`$ is odd in each coupling, $`M_1^o=M_2^o=B[1+O(\eta)]`$, $`M_i^e=O(\eta B)`$. In (5.3) the field state of the flipped branch differs at first order in $`g_i`$ by a vector of squared norm $`O(\eta^2)`$ per object, so $`c_i^2=\tau_i^2[1+O(N_f\eta^2)]`$ and $`\mu_i=ng_i\tau_i^{-2}[1+O(N_f\eta^2)]`$; in particular $`\mathrm{sign}\mu_i=\mathrm{sign}g_i`$.

### Step 2. Band contract and selected level (item 1)

*Group and distances.* Each motion term conserves $`x_i`$ plus the branch index mod 2, so $`r+s_1+s_2`$ mod 2 is conserved ($`s_i=0`$ on the selected, $`1`$ on the flipped branch). $`\mathcal G`$ ($`r`$ even, $`s_1=s_2=0`$) lies in the class with $`r+s_1+s_2`$ even, together with its field excitations, the single flips at odd $`r`$ and the joint flip at even $`r`$. The class with odd $`r`$ has its own selected level, and everything below holds for either. The motion terms connect $`\mathcal G`$ at first order only to the single flips; the joint flip is reached at second order. Its held levels are $`E_{--}(r')=N_f\varepsilon_0(-g_1\,\text{at}\,j,-g_2\,\text{at}\,l)`$, and by the expansion of Step 1, whose second-order terms are even under the joint flip, $`E(r)-E_{--}(r')=2n(g_1+g_2)+B(r)-B(r')+O(n\lvert g\rvert\eta^2)`$. $`B`$ ranges over an interval of length $`\lvert F\rvert L/4=\frac32\lvert B(0)\rvert[1+O(L^{-2})]`$ ($`V`$ below), so the two held spectra are at distance $`d_\times=2n\lvert g_1+g_2\rvert-\frac32\lvert B(0)\rvert`$ at leading order. The single flips are at $`2n\lvert g_i\rvert[1+O(\eta)]`$ ((5.4) for each body), and the excited field states of the flipped sectors at $`\varepsilon_1-2n\sum_{\rm flipped}g_i`$; this gives $`d`$ of S2.

Under S2 the derivation of (2.10) applies unchanged: it uses only that $`\mathcal G`$ is isolated in the held spectrum and that each motion term moves one body by one place and flips its branch; the full grip changes only the held levels ($`\theta=0`$ in (5.1)). So (2.10) holds with error $`O(\tau^4/d^3)`$; the terms through the joint flip are of fourth order, with denominators $`\ge d`$, and belong to it. Where (N×) fails, in particular for $`g_1=-g_2`$, where reflection maps the selected held spectrum onto the jointly flipped one, $`\mathcal G`$ is not isolated and the scalar band contract is not used; such descriptions are outside the class. With (2.1) the ansatz $`\psi(j,l)=e^{iPj}g(l-j)`$ gives (6.1) unchanged (the double step of $`b_1`$ carries the relative field coordinates: phase $`e^{-2iP}`$).

*Amplitudes.* The double step of $`b_i`$ across the relative bond $`\rho`$ passes through the held level with $`b_i`$ flipped at relative place $`\rho`$. Since $`M(-g_i)=-M_i^o+M_i^e`$, the gap from the held level at $`r=\rho\mp1`$ is
```math
\Delta_i(r;\rho)=2\sigma_iR_i+M_i^o(r)+M_i^o(\rho)+M_i^e(r)-M_i^e(\rho).\qquad\text{(7.2)}
```
Second-order Schrieffer–Wolff with symmetrized denominators gives $`\kappa_i(\rho)=-\frac{\tau_i^2a_i^2}8\big[\Delta_i(\rho-1;\rho)^{-1}+\Delta_i(\rho+1;\rho)^{-1}\big]`$, up to the excited-state term of (5.3); for $`M=0`$ this is $`\bar\kappa_i=-1/(8\mu_i)`$ of (5.3). The difference $`-1/(8\kappa_i(\rho))-\mu_i`$ vanishes with the partner's coupling $`g_j`$. Its first order is the shift of the denominators by $`M_i^o=O(\sigma_iR_i\eta_j)`$. The next terms are relative $`O(\eta)`$ to it: the second order of this expansion, relative $`O(\eta_j)`$, and the partner's change of the field overlaps, relative $`O(\eta_i)`$. The $`M_i^e`$ terms combine to a second difference, which is negligible. So the **local inertia** of $`b_i`$ is
```math
m_i(\rho):=-\frac1{8\kappa_i(\rho)}=\mu_i+\frac{\bar M_i^o(\rho)}{c_i^2}+O(\lvert\mu_i\rvert\eta\eta_j)=\mu_i+\frac{\bar B(\rho)}{\tau_i^2}\big[1+O(\eta)\big].\qquad\text{(7.3)}
```
The partner's mark changes each body's local inertia by the bond average of the mutual term over that body's own $`\tau_i^2`$. The same gaps give $`d_i(r)=-2\bar\kappa_i[1-\bar M_i^o(r)/\sigma_iR_i]`$. (6.5) and (6.6) hold: they use only the form (6.1), a real $`f_0`$ at $`P=0`$, and Hellmann–Feynman in the bond magnitudes and the flux.

*Bound level.* $`U(r)-U(0)=V(r)[1+O(\eta,\tau^2/d^2)]`$ with $`V(r):=B(r)-B(0)=F\,r(L-r)/L`$ on $`0\le r\le L`$. Near the edge $`P=0`$, $`k=0`$ of the relative band, (6.1) has relative hop $`\bar t_0=-\frac18(\mu_1^{-1}+\mu_2^{-1})`$: the selected level is the lowest if $`\bar t_0<0`$, the highest if $`\bar t_0>0`$. It is bound at $`r\approx0`$ iff the extremum of $`V`$ at $`r=0`$ pushes it out of the band: a minimum ($`F>0`$) for the lowest, a maximum ($`F<0`$) for the highest, i.e. $`g_1g_2(\mu_1^{-1}+\mu_2^{-1})>0`$. With Step 1, in the class (S2 with (N×)):
```math
\text{selected level bound}\iff\tau_1^2g_2+\tau_2^2g_1>0,\qquad g_i=\sigma_i\gamma_iM_i .\qquad\text{(7.4)}
```
Both $`g_i>0`$: bound, lowest. Both $`g_i<0`$: not bound. Opposite signs: bound (highest) iff the inequality holds. Otherwise the extreme level sits at $`r\approx L/2`$, where $`V`$ has its other extremum (control: case D of the code). In the continuum limit of (6.1), $`-4\lvert\bar t_0\rvert\partial_r^2+\lvert F\rvert\lvert r\rvert`$, the width is $`w`$ of S4 and $`1-\beta_0=O(w^{-2})`$ for $`w\gg1`$ (for $`w\ll1`$, $`\beta_0=O(w^3)`$); $`f_0>0`$ (Perron–Frobenius), so $`b_0>0`$ and $`0<\beta_0\le1`$.

### Step 3. Inertia of the pair (item 2)

By (7.3), $`m(\rho)=\mu_1+\mu_2+\delta m(\rho)`$, $`\delta m=\sum_i\bar M_i^o/c_i^2+e(\rho)`$, $`e=O(\sum_i\lvert\mu_i\rvert\eta\eta_j)`$. In (6.6), $`1/\sum_\rho b_0/m`$ is exact in the constant part of $`\delta m`$, which it divides by $`\beta_0`$: a constant $`e`$ shifts $`1/E_b''(0)`$ by $`e/\beta_0`$. The variation of $`\delta m`$ over the pair enters first, and at second order as $`-\mathrm{Var}(\delta m)/((\mu_1+\mu_2)\beta_0)`$ (variance in the weights $`b_0/\beta_0`$), relative $`O(\eta w^2/L^2)`$ to the first-order term. The band contract differs from the exact contract by relative $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ in the $`r`$-dependent part of the hops. In their constant part it differs as for each body alone, and the exact $`\mu_i`$ absorb this up to relative $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ of the lattice part. Hence
```math
D_0=D^{\rm lat}\Big[1+O\Big(\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\Big)\Big]+D^{\rm mark}\Big[1+O\Big(\eta,\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\Big)\Big]-\frac{\rho_L}{E''_{\rm line}}+O\Big(\frac{\rho_L^2}{E''_{\rm line}}\Big),\quad D^{\rm lat}=(\mu_1+\mu_2)\frac{1-\beta_0}{\beta_0},\quad D^{\rm mark}=\frac1{\beta_0^2}\sum_\rho b_0\sum_i\frac{\bar M_i^o}{c_i^2}=\Big(\frac1{\tau_1^2}+\frac1{\tau_2^2}\Big)\frac{\sum_\rho b_0\bar B}{\beta_0^2}\big[1+O(\eta)\big].\qquad\text{(7.5)}
```
Both parts carry the factor $`1/\beta_0`$: $`\sum_\rho b_0\bar B=\beta_0B(0)+\sum_\rho b_0\bar V`$ with $`\lvert\sum_\rho b_0\bar V\rvert/\beta_0=O(\lvert F\rvert\max(w,1))`$, so $`\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert=(\tau_1^{-2}+\tau_2^{-2})\lvert B(0)\rvert\beta_0^{-1}[1+O(\eta,\max(w,1)/L)]`$. Each remainder is relative to the part it corrects, so (7.5) holds for small $`\beta_0`$ as well. This is (6.8) with $`s_c=+1`$ and $`c_i=\tau_i`$, the full-grip speeds of (5.7), plus the flux remainder of (6.6). $`D^{\rm lat}`$ comes from the lattice form of the bands, $`D^{\rm mark}`$ from the mutual term. Each body's inertia counts the part of $`M`$ odd in its own coupling, and at leading order this is all of $`M`$ for each body.

*Flux remainder.* By (6.6), $`E_b''(0)=E''_{\rm line}(1+\rho_L)`$ with $`\rho_L:=\tilde\Phi'(0)^2\partial^2_{\tilde\Phi}\epsilon_0/E''_{\rm line}`$. This term comes from circuits of the relative motion around the ring, and $`\tilde\Phi'(0)^2=O(L^2)`$ outside the tuned case.
- For $`w\gg1`$ the continuum bound state (Step 2) decays as $`\exp[-\frac23(\lvert r\rvert/w)^{3/2}]`$, and a circuit passes $`r\approx\frac L2`$. So $`\lvert\rho_L\rvert=O(e^{-c(L/w)^{3/2}})`$ up to powers of $`L/w`$, with $`c>0`$.
- For $`w\lesssim1`$ the locator expansion about $`r=0`$ gives the lowest circuit. It has $`\frac L2`$ hops $`\simeq\bar t_0`$ and $`\frac L2-1`$ denominators $`\lvert V(2k)\rvert\ge\lvert F\rvert`$, so $`\partial^2_{\tilde\Phi}\epsilon_0=O(\lvert\bar t_0\rvert^{L/2}/\lvert F\rvert^{L/2-1})`$. With $`E''_{\rm line}=O(\bar t_0^2/\lvert F\rvert)`$ ($`b_0(\pm1)\simeq\lvert\bar t_0/V(2)\rvert`$), this gives $`\lvert\rho_L\rvert=O(L^2(w^3/4)^{L/2-2})`$. This is $`O(w^3)`$ at $`L=6`$ and not exponentially small (control F of the code).

Against the mutual part of the inertia the flux term is $`\varepsilon_L:=\lvert\rho_L\rvert/(E''_{\rm line}\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert)`$. Since $`E''_{\rm line}=\beta_0(\mu_1+\mu_2)^{-1}[1+O(\eta)]`$ and $`\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert\ge2\eta_{\min}\lvert\mu_1+\mu_2\rvert\beta_0^{-1}[1+O(\eta,\max(w,1)/L)]`$ (from $`\lvert g_1g_2\rvert(\tau_1^{-2}+\tau_2^{-2})\ge\min_i\lvert g_i\rvert\sum_i\lvert g_i\rvert\tau_i^{-2}`$), we get $`\varepsilon_L\le\lvert\rho_L\rvert/(2\eta_{\min})[1+O(\eta,\max(w,1)/L)]`$. At small $`L`$ these corrections are not small (control F at $`L=6`$: $`\varepsilon_L=1.9`$). Wherever $`w`$ may be small, $`\rho_L`$ and $`\varepsilon_L`$ are kept explicit.

### Step 4. Cost of the bond and inertia against cost (item 3)

With $`f_0`$ real, $`E_b(0)=\sum_rf_0^2U+2\sum_\rho b_0\,t(\rho;0)`$, $`E_i(0)=E_i^{\rm held}+\bar d_i+2\bar\kappa_i`$, $`E^{\rm held}_{\rm pair}(r)=E_1^{\rm held}+E_2^{\rm held}-E_\varnothing+M(r)`$ (definition (4.2)). The partner shifts $`d_i`$ and $`\kappa_i`$ by the relative amount $`-\bar M_i^o/\sigma_iR_i`$ (Step 2), and $`\bar d_i=-2\bar\kappa_i`$, so these shifts cancel up to the factor $`1-\beta_0`$:
```math
\Delta E_0=\langle M\rangle_0+K_0\big[1+O(\eta)\big],\qquad K_0=2\bar t_0(\beta_0-1)=\tfrac14(\mu_1^{-1}+\mu_2^{-1})(1-\beta_0).\qquad\text{(7.6)}
```
**(i)** Since $`(\mu_1+\mu_2)/(\mu_1^{-1}+\mu_2^{-1})=\mu_1\mu_2`$, $`D^{\rm lat}=4\mu_1\mu_2K_0/\beta_0`$ exactly. By Step 3, $`\sum_\rho b_0\bar B/\beta_0=\langle M\rangle_0+O(\eta\lvert B(0)\rvert,\lvert F\rvert w)`$. With $`v=\tau_i`$, the speed of (5.7) of either body, and $`j\neq i`$:
```math
D_0-\frac{\Delta E_0}{\tau_i^2}=\frac{4\mu_1\mu_2}{\beta_0}K_0\Big[1-\frac{\beta_0}{4\mu_1\mu_2\tau_i^2}\Big]+\frac{\langle M\rangle_0}{\beta_0}\Big[\frac1{\tau_j^2}+\frac{1-\beta_0}{\tau_i^2}\Big]-\frac{\rho_L}{E''_{\rm line}}+O\Big(\Big(\eta+\frac wL+\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\Big)\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert+\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\lvert D^{\rm lat}\rvert+\frac{\rho_L^2}{E''_{\rm line}}\Big),\qquad\lvert4\mu_1\mu_2\tau_i^2\rvert=\frac{(2n\lvert g_1\rvert)(2n\lvert g_2\rvert)}{\tau_j^2}\ge\frac{d^2}{\tau_j^2}\gg1.\qquad\text{(7.7)}
```
The lattice part exceeds $`K_0/v^2`$ by a factor $`\ge d^2/\tau_j^2`$, and the mark part exceeds $`\langle M\rangle_0/v^2`$ by $`\langle M\rangle_0[\tau_j^{-2}+(1-\beta_0)\tau_i^{-2}]/\beta_0`$. For $`g_1g_2<0`$ both displayed terms are positive ($`\mu_1\mu_2K_0>0`$, $`\langle M\rangle_0>0`$). So (i) fails unless the remainder and the flux term are as large as both of them, which needs $`\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2=O(\eta+w/L+\tau^2/d^2)`$ and $`\lvert D^{\rm lat}\rvert`$ small against $`\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert`$: the limit of (7.8). For $`g_1,g_2>0`$ the two terms have opposite signs, and (i) holds only on the surface where they cancel, a tuned relation between $`1-\beta_0`$ and $`\eta`$. Elsewhere, no.

**(ii)** In S5, by (7.5), $`\lvert D^{\rm lat}\rvert=\delta_m\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert[1+O(\eta,w/L)]`$: the $`\beta_0`$ factors cancel, and the mutual term dominates the inertia. With the tuned case excluded, $`\lvert\mu_1+\mu_2\rvert=\lvert\mu_1^{-1}+\mu_2^{-1}\rvert\lvert\mu_1\mu_2\rvert\ge c\max_i\lvert\mu_i\rvert\ge\frac c2\sum_i\lvert\mu_i\rvert`$. With $`\lvert\mu_i\rvert=n\lvert g_i\rvert\tau_i^{-2}`$ and $`\lvert B(0)\rvert=2n\min_i\lvert g_i\rvert\,\eta`$ this gives $`1-\beta_0\le4\eta\delta_m/c\ll\eta`$; so $`w\gg1`$ and $`\beta_0=1-O(\eta\delta_m)`$. The converse fails: for $`\tau_1=\tau_2`$, $`0<g_2\ll g_1`$, $`\delta_m\simeq(1-\beta_0)/(4\eta_2)`$, which needs $`1-\beta_0\ll\eta_2`$. By (7.7), $`\lvert K_0\rvert=\beta_0\lvert D^{\rm lat}\rvert/\lvert4\mu_1\mu_2\rvert\le2\delta_m(\tau_{\max}^2/d^2)\lvert B(0)\rvert[1+O(\eta)]`$, so $`\Delta E_0=\langle M\rangle_0[1+O(\delta_m\tau^2/d^2)]`$. For $`w\gg1`$ the weights $`b_0/\beta_0`$ and $`f_0^2`$ differ by relative $`O(w^{-2})`$, so $`\sum_\rho b_0\bar B/\beta_0=\langle M\rangle_0[1+O(\eta)]`$. Against the mutual scale $`\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert`$ the omitted terms are then: the lattice part, $`O(\delta_m)`$; its remainder, $`O(\delta_m\tau^2/d^2)`$; the mark remainder, $`O(\eta,\tau^2/d^2)`$; the ring remainder, $`\varepsilon_L`$, which is $`O(e^{-c(L/w)^{3/2}}/\eta_{\min})`$ since S5 implies $`w\gg1`$; $`1/\beta_0-1=O(\eta\delta_m)`$; and $`K_0`$ in $`\Delta E_0`$, $`O(\delta_m\tau^2/d^2)`$. Hence
```math
D_0=\Big(\frac1{\tau_1^2}+\frac1{\tau_2^2}\Big)\Delta E_0\Big[1+O\Big(\eta,\delta_m,\frac{\tau^2}{d^2},\varepsilon_L\Big)\Big];\qquad\text{equal speeds }\tau_1=\tau_2=v:\ D_0=\frac{2\Delta E_0}{v^2}.\qquad\text{(7.8)}
```
The binding cost has inertia, but $`(\tau_1^{-2}+\tau_2^{-2})\Delta E_0\ne\Delta E_0/\tau_i^2`$: the mutual term is odd in both couplings, so it enters both half-gaps (7.3) but the cost only once. It approaches $`\Delta E_0/\tau_i^2`$ only for $`\tau_i/\tau_j\to0`$, with relative deviation $`\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2`$.

### Step 5. Weight and fall (item 4)

*Fixed source and centre contract.* The source $`g_s\Phi_{x_s}`$ of S6 breaks (2.1), so it has no $`P`$ sectors. Let $`\Psi_P`$, $`P\in\frac{2\pi}L\mathbb Z_L`$, be the eigenvectors of the selected level without source. Write them as $`\Psi_P=L^{-1/2}\sum e^{iPX}u_P`$, with the midpoint $`X=j+h/2`$ ($`h\in(-\frac L2,\frac L2]`$ the representative of $`r`$) and the internal state $`u_P`$ (branches, $`r`$, field place relative to $`X`$). The phases are fixed by $`\langle u_P|u_0\rangle>0`$. The selected band is isolated from the other levels of the relative motion by a gap $`\Delta_{\rm rel}>0`$. At first order in $`g_s`$, projection on it gives the centre contract
```math
h_c=E_b(\hat P)+V,\qquad\langle X|V|X'\rangle=g_s\langle X|\Phi_{x_s}|X'\rangle,\qquad|X\rangle:=L^{-1/2}\textstyle\sum_Pe^{-iPX}\Psi_P,\qquad\text{(7.14)}
```
up to $`O(g_s^2/\Delta_{\rm rel})`$ from the other levels. The $`P=0`$ symbol $`W_{\rm ex}(X):=\sum_{X'}\langle X|V|X'\rangle=g_s\sum_Pe^{iPX}\langle\Psi_P|\Phi_{x_s}|\Psi_0\rangle`$ is the potential of the centre at rest. The rest of $`V`$ comes from the $`P`$-dependence of $`u_P`$ and acts as a position-dependent inertia. It changes the acceleration from rest of a start with real envelope of width $`w_X\gg w`$ at relative $`O(w^2/w_X^2)`$. So (5.14) and (6.13) apply to $`h_c`$, with $`W_{\rm ex}`$ as the weight.

*Relation to the internal density.* Inserting $`\Psi_P`$ gives $`W_{\rm ex}(X)=g_s\sum_aG(a;x_s-X-a)`$, where $`G(a;k):=\frac1L\sum_Pe^{-iPa}\sum u_P^*u_0\vert_k`$ (sum over branches and $`r`$, field place $`k`$ from the midpoint). The $`P=0`$ part is $`\sum_aG(a;k)=\rho_0(k)`$, the field density at distance $`k`$ from the midpoint in the $`P=0`$ eigenvector, i.e. the internal density conditioned on the midpoint. Since $`u_P`$ is smooth in $`P`$, $`G`$ has a range of a few places in $`a`$. For $`\lvert k\rvert\gg w`$, a Taylor expansion in $`a`$ gives $`W_{\rm ex}=g_s[\rho_0-\alpha'+\frac12\beta''-\dots](x_s-X)`$ with $`\alpha:=\sum_aaG`$ and $`\beta:=\sum_aa^2G`$.

At first order in the couplings, each component of $`u`$ (fixed branches and $`r`$) has the far density $`n-\frac{2n}{t_f}\sum_bc_b\ell(k-z_b)`$ of its held field (Step 1), with couplings $`c_b=\pm g_i`$ at $`z_b`$. This density is quadratic in $`k`$ ($`\ell''=\frac1L`$). So $`\beta''`$ is constant and drops from the gradient, and $`\alpha''=-\frac{2n}{t_fL}\sum_{\rm comp}(\sum_bc_b)\,a_1^{\rm comp}`$, where $`a_1^{\rm comp}`$ is the first moment of $`G`$ in that component. On $`\mathcal G`$ every component has total coupling $`g_1+g_2`$, and $`\sum a_1=0`$ by the phase choice. So only the flipped components contribute: their weight is $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ and their $`a_1=O(1)`$. Hence, with $`W_0(X):=g_s\rho_0(x_s-X)`$,
```math
W_{\rm ex}'(X)=W_0'(X)+O\Big(\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\frac{n\lvert g_s\rvert\max_i\lvert g_i\rvert}{t_fL}\Big)+O\Big(\frac{w^2}{L^2}\frac{n\lvert g_s\rvert\max_ig_i^2\,\ell_0}{t_f^2}\Big).\qquad\text{(7.15)}
```
The last term is the second order in the couplings, whose far density is quartic. Relative to the bond part $`W_b'`$ below ($`\lvert W_b'\rvert\simeq4\kappa_s^{3/2}n\lvert g_sg_1g_2\rvert\ell_0/t_f^2`$), the two remainders are $`O(\tau^2/(d^2\eta_{\min}L\kappa_s^{3/2}))`$ and $`O((\eta/\eta_{\min})(w/(L\kappa_s))^2)`$. The same holds for one body, with its place as centre. $`W_0`$ is a property of the $`P=0`$ eigenvector. The code computes it from the density, and cross-checks it with the Hellmann–Feynman shift under the operator $`g_s\sum_{j,l}\lvert j,l\rangle\langle j,l\rvert\otimes\Phi_{X(j,l)+s}`$. This operator is an auxiliary mathematical probe on the sector space: it couples both bodies and the field and is not a contract of the description.

*Content of $`W_0`$.* $`\rho_0`$ (for odd $`r`$, the two places at distance $`k\mp\frac12`$ averaged) contains the held densities and, through the flipped components, the change of the hops: the kinetic contribution. In the band contract in laboratory coordinates the source enters (2.10) through the held blocks at first order in $`g_s`$, so (6.13) carries over: $`W_0'=\sum_rf_0^2(\partial_1+\partial_2)W_{\rm pair}`$ plus the kinetic part. The normalized weights $`b_0/m`$ of the first form of (6.13) give the same constant part as $`f_0^2`$, and in the $`r`$-dependent part $`\langle V\rangle_0`$ they differ by relative $`O(1-\beta_0,\eta)`$. By (7.3) with the source in place of the partner, $`\kappa_i,d_i`$ change by the relative amount $`-\phi`$ ($`M^o_{is}=ng_i\phi`$). So the kinetic energies weigh $`-(\text{kinetic energy})\,\phi'`$. A body alone at $`P=0`$ has none ($`\bar d_i+2\bar\kappa_i=0`$), and the bond part is $`-K_0\phi'`$. All such terms are relative $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ against $`ng\phi'`$.

*Lattice identities.* For $`1\le x\le L-1`$: $`\ell'(x)=-\frac12(1-\frac{2x}L)`$; $`(\ell^2)'=(2\ell+\frac1L)\ell'`$ (as $`\Lambda\ell=-\frac1L`$ there); and since $`\Lambda\ell^{(2)}=\ell`$, $`\ell^{(2)}`$ is even about $`0`$ and $`L/2`$, the cubic $`-\frac{Lx}{12}+\frac{x^2}4-\frac{x^3}{6L}=\frac{x(L-x)}{6}\ell'(x)`$ is $`(\ell^{(2)})'`$. Inserting these into the derivative of $`2\ell_0\ell+\ell^2-\frac2L\ell^{(2)}`$ in (4.5) gives $`4\ell_0\kappa_s\ell'`$ with $`\kappa_s`$ below, and $`\phi'=-\frac{2g_s}{t_f}\ell'`$:
```math
W_i'(X)=\phi'\big[ng_i+2\kappa_sC_i^{(2)}\big],\qquad C_i^{(2)}:=-\frac{ng_i^2\ell_0}{t_f},\qquad\kappa_s:=\frac{L^2(1-2u)^2+2}{L^2-1}=(1-2u)^2+O(L^{-2}),\qquad\text{(7.9)}
```
up to relative $`O(\eta^2)`$. $`C_i^{(2)}`$ is the second-order even part of the cost; it is weighed with $`2\kappa_s`$ but has no inertia by (5.3).

*Bond.* In (4.7) at $`x_{1,2}=X\mp r/2`$: $`f_0`$ is even in $`r`$ at $`P=0`$ (as $`V`$ and $`\kappa_i`$ are), so $`\langle r\rangle_0=0`$. As $`\phi`$ is quadratic away from $`x_s`$, $`\partial_X[W_b(X;r)-W_b(X;0)]=V(r)\phi'(X)+O(\lvert F\phi'\rvert r^2/L)`$ for $`\lvert r\rvert<s`$, and $`W_b(X;0)`$ is (4.5) with $`g_i^2\to2g_1g_2`$. So, with the kinetic part,
```math
W_b':=W_0'-W_1'-W_2'=\phi'\big[2\kappa_sB(0)+\langle V\rangle_0-K_0\big]+O\big(\eta\lvert B(0)\phi'\rvert\big)+O\big(\lvert F\phi'\rvert w^2/L\big).\qquad\text{(7.10)}
```
The first remainder is the next order of the bond weight in the couplings. Like $`W_b'`$ it is odd about the antipode, hence proportional to $`\phi'`$. $`\langle V\rangle_0=O(\lvert B(0)\rvert w/L)`$ and $`K_0`$ are kept; (7.10) needs no relation between $`\kappa_s`$ and the small parameters.

*Fall factors.* By (5.14), (6.13) applied to $`h_c`$ (7.14), with $`W_{\rm ex}'=W_0'`$ up to (7.15), $`\Gamma_i:=E_i''(0)W_i'=W_i'/\mu_i`$ and $`\Gamma_{\rm pair}:=E_b''(0)W_0'`$ set the acceleration from rest $`-\Gamma`$ of a smooth start in the fixed source. With $`\Gamma_b:=W_b'/D_0`$ and $`1/E_b''=\mu_1+\mu_2+D_0`$, exactly,
```math
\Gamma_{\rm pair}=\frac{\mu_1\Gamma_1+\mu_2\Gamma_2+D_0\Gamma_b}{\mu_1+\mu_2+D_0},\qquad\Gamma_i=\tau_i^2\phi'\Big[1-\frac{2\kappa_sg_i\ell_0}{t_f}+O\Big(\eta^2,N_f\eta^2,\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\Big)\Big].\qquad\text{(7.11)}
```
*Leading order.* $`W_b'`$ and $`D^{\rm mark}`$ are $`O(\eta)`$; the lattice inertia has weight $`-K_0\phi'=-\beta_0D^{\rm lat}\phi'/(4\mu_1\mu_2)`$, which is $`O(\tau^2/d^2)`$ against $`\tau^2\phi'D^{\rm lat}`$. Hence, with $`E_b''=E''_{\rm line}(1+\rho_L)`$ (Step 3), $`\Gamma_{\rm pair}=(1+\rho_L)\beta_0\bar\Gamma[1+O(\eta,\tau^2/d^2)]`$ for every $`w`$ of S4, with $`\bar\Gamma:=\frac{\mu_1\Gamma_1+\mu_2\Gamma_2}{\mu_1+\mu_2}=\frac{\mu_1\tau_1^2+\mu_2\tau_2^2}{\mu_1+\mu_2}\phi'[1+O(\eta)]`$. So $`\Gamma_{\rm pair}=\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2`$ at leading order (with $`1-\beta_0\to0`$, hence $`w\gg1`$ and $`\rho_L\to0`$) iff $`\tau_1=\tau_2`$. At $`w\lesssim1`$ and small $`L`$, the factor $`1+\rho_L`$ is not close to 1 (control F). For $`\tau_1\ne\tau_2`$ the bodies already fall differently; $`1-\beta_0`$ lowers the pair's fall, since the internal kinetic energy has inertia but no weight at this order.

*Order of the mutual term (S5, S6).* By (7.10), (7.8), with $`\langle V\rangle_0/(2\kappa_sB(0))=O(w/(L\kappa_s))`$, $`K_0/(\kappa_sB(0))=O(\delta_m\tau^2/(\kappa_sd^2))`$ (Step 4) and $`\Delta E_0=B(0)[1+O(\eta,w/L)]`$:
```math
\Gamma_b=\frac{2\kappa_s\,\phi'}{\tau_1^{-2}+\tau_2^{-2}}\Big[1+O\Big(\frac\eta{\kappa_s},\frac w{L\kappa_s},\delta_m,\frac{\tau^2}{d^2},\frac{\delta_m\tau^2}{\kappa_sd^2},\frac{\tau^2}{d^2\eta_{\min}L\kappa_s^{3/2}},\varepsilon_L\Big)\Big],\qquad \Gamma_{\rm pair}-\bar\Gamma\overset{\tau_1=\tau_2=\tau}{=}4(1-\kappa_s)\frac{g_1g_2\ell_0}{t_f(g_1+g_2)}\tau^2\phi'.\qquad\text{(7.12)}
```
The sixth remainder is the first one of (7.15) for the fixed source. The second one, $`O((\eta/\eta_{\min})(w/(L\kappa_s))^2)`$, is below $`w/(L\kappa_s)`$ unless the couplings differ by a factor $`\gtrsim L\kappa_s/w`$. Under the hierarchy of S6, and with $`\varepsilon_L\ll1`$ (S5 gives $`\varepsilon_L=O(e^{-c(L/w)^{3/2}}/\eta_{\min})`$), every remainder is small; the $`f_0^2`$ and $`b_0/m`$ forms of $`W_0'`$ differ by relative $`O((1-\beta_0)w/(L\kappa_s))`$ of $`\Gamma_b`$. For equal speeds the bond's weight and inertia both carry the factor 2 of (7.8). The weight also carries $`\kappa_s=1-\frac{4s(L-s)-3}{L^2-1}<1`$ for $`1\le s\le L/2`$, so $`\Gamma_b=\kappa_s\tau^2\phi'`$ against $`\Gamma_i=\tau^2\phi'`$ at leading order. At leading order the binding cost is weighed in the same proportion as its inertia only in the limit $`u\to0`$ ($`w\ll s\ll L`$), where $`\Gamma_b/\Gamma_i=1-4u+O(u^2)`$ up to the remainders of (7.11), (7.12). At finite $`u`$, where $`1-\kappa_s\simeq4u`$ exceeds these remainders, it is weighed less. The second equation of (7.12) is the first order of the difference, from (7.11) with $`D_0\simeq2B(0)/\tau^2`$, $`\Gamma_b-\bar\Gamma\simeq(\kappa_s-1)\tau^2\phi'`$, $`\mu_1+\mu_2\simeq n(g_1+g_2)/\tau^2`$. Besides this, $`\bar\Gamma-\Gamma_i=O(\eta)`$ for $`g_1\ne g_2`$ from the self terms of (7.11).

*One body and both bodies.* By (7.11), (7.12), $`\Gamma_b/\Gamma_i=2\kappa_s/(1+\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2)`$ at leading order. Equality with both bodies at once ($`\Gamma_b=\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2`$, one ratio of weight to inertia for bond and bodies) needs $`\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2`$, i.e. $`\tau_1=\tau_2`$ at leading order, and then $`\kappa_s=1`$, i.e. $`u\to0`$. Equality with body $`i`$ alone holds at $`\kappa_s=\frac12(1+\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2)`$. This value lies in $`(\frac12,1)`$, and is reached at one source distance, $`u\simeq\frac12(1-\sqrt{\kappa_s})`$, iff $`\tau_i<\tau_j`$ (inside S6 if $`1-\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2\gg w/L`$); for $`\tau_i\ge\tau_j`$ it is not reached at any $`u>0`$. There $`\Gamma_b/\Gamma_j=\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2<1`$: the bond shares the proportion of the slower body only, at a tuned source position.

### Step 6. The hopping of the field (item 5)

At fixed $`n,\gamma_i,M_i,\tau_i`$ (so fixed $`g_i`$, $`\mu_i`$) and leading order: $`B,\langle M\rangle_0,D^{\rm mark},W_b'/\phi'\propto t_f^{-1}`$; $`F\propto t_f^{-1}`$, so $`w\propto t_f^{1/3}`$, and for $`w\gg1`$, $`1-\beta_0\propto t_f^{-2/3}`$, $`K_0,\langle V\rangle_0,D^{\rm lat}\propto t_f^{-2/3}`$; $`\phi'\propto g_s/t_f`$, $`\Gamma_i/\phi'`$ and $`\mu_i`$ are independent of $`t_f`$. Hence
```math
\frac{D^{\rm lat}}{\lvert D^{\rm mark}\rvert}\propto t_f^{1/3},\qquad \frac{D^{\rm mark}}{\Delta E_0}\to\frac1{\tau_1^2}+\frac1{\tau_2^2},\quad \frac{\Gamma_b}{\Gamma_i}\to\frac{2\kappa_s}{1+\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2}\quad\text{independent of }t_f.\qquad\text{(7.13)}
```
A stiffer field weakens the bond ($`\propto t_f^{-1}`$) and widens it, and S5 lies at the small-$`t_f`$ end, bounded by $`\eta\propto t_f^{-1}\ll1`$ and by $`\varepsilon_1\propto t_f`$ in S2. No value of $`t_f`$ restores the proportions of (7.8) and (7.12).

The lattice term exceeds $`K_0/v^2`$ by a factor $`\ge d^2/\tau_j^2`$. For $`g_1g_2<0`$ both terms are positive, and (i) fails except in the limit of (7.8). For $`g_1,g_2>0`$ it holds only on a tuned surface.
- **(7.8)** Item 3 (ii), negative: in S5 ($`\delta_m\ll1`$, which implies $`1-\beta_0\ll\eta`$ and $`w\gg1`$), $`D_0=(\tau_1^{-2}+\tau_2^{-2})\Delta E_0[1+O(\eta,\delta_m,\tau^2/d^2,\varepsilon_L)]`$, i.e. $`2\Delta E_0/v^2`$ for $`\tau_1=\tau_2=v`$; here $`\varepsilon_L\le\lvert\rho_L\rvert/(2\eta_{\min})[1+O(\eta,w/L)]`$. It approaches $`\Delta E_0/\tau_i^2`$ only as $`\tau_i/\tau_j\to0`$.
- **(7.14)** At first order in $`g_s`$, the fixed held source $`g_s\Phi_{x_s}`$ in laboratory coordinates acts on the isolated selected band as the centre contract $`h_c=E_b(\hat P)+V`$, up to $`O(g_s^2/\Delta_{\rm rel})`$. The $`P=0`$ symbol $`W_{\rm ex}`$ of $`V`$ is the weight of the centre at rest; the rest of $`V`$ changes the acceleration from rest of a start of width $`w_X\gg w`$ at relative $`O(w^2/w_X^2)`$.
- **(7.15)** For $`s\gg w`$: $`W_{\rm ex}'=W_0'+O(\frac{\tau^2}{d^2}\frac{n\lvert g_s\rvert\max\lvert g_i\rvert}{t_fL})+O(\frac{w^2}{L^2}\frac{n\lvert g_s\rvert\max g_i^2\ell_0}{t_f^2})`$, with $`W_0(X)=g_s\rho_0(x_s-X)`$ and $`\rho_0`$ the field density at distance $`x_s-X`$ from the midpoint in the $`P=0`$ eigenvector. Relative to the bond weight the remainders are $`O(\tau^2/(d^2\eta_{\min}L\kappa_s^{3/2}))`$ and $`O((\eta/\eta_{\min})(w/(L\kappa_s))^2)`$. The same holds for one body. The midpoint-following operator of the code is an auxiliary probe, not a contract of the description.
- **(7.9), (7.10)** In S6: $`W_i'=\phi'[ng_i+2\kappa_sC_i^{(2)}]`$ up to relative $`O(\eta^2)`$, with $`\kappa_s=(L^2(1-2u)^2+2)/(L^2-1)`$. $`W_b'=\phi'[2\kappa_sB(0)+\langle V\rangle_0-K_0]+O(\eta\lvert B(0)\phi'\rvert)+O(\lvert F\phi'\rvert w^2/L)`$. Here $`W_0=g_s\rho_0(x_s-X)`$ of (7.15), kinetic contribution included.
- **(7.11)** Exact: $`\Gamma_{\rm pair}=(\mu_1\Gamma_1+\mu_2\Gamma_2+D_0\Gamma_b)/(\mu_1+\mu_2+D_0)`$, and $`\Gamma_i=\tau_i^2\phi'[1-2\kappa_sg_i\ell_0/t_f+O(\eta^2,N_f\eta^2,\tau^2/d^2)]`$. For every $`w`$ of S4, $`\Gamma_{\rm pair}=(1+\rho_L)\beta_0\bar\Gamma[1+O(\eta,\tau^2/d^2)]`$. For $`1-\beta_0\to0`$ (so $`w\gg1`$, $`\rho_L\to0`$) this equals $`\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2`$ iff $`\tau_1=\tau_2`$. Controls: $`\tau_1\ne\tau_2`$; for $`w\lesssim1`$ at small $`L`$, $`\rho_L`$ is not negligible (F).
- **(7.12)** In S5 and S6 ($`\eta\ll\kappa_s`$, $`w/L\ll\kappa_s`$, $`\delta_m\tau^2/d^2\ll\kappa_s`$, $`\tau^2/(d^2\eta_{\min}L)\ll\kappa_s^{3/2}`$), for the fixed source: $`\Gamma_b=2\kappa_s\phi'/(\tau_1^{-2}+\tau_2^{-2})\,[1+O(\eta/\kappa_s,w/(L\kappa_s),\delta_m,\tau^2/d^2,\delta_m\tau^2/(\kappa_sd^2),\tau^2/(d^2\eta_{\min}L\kappa_s^{3/2}),\varepsilon_L)]`$. For $`\tau_1=\tau_2`$, at leading order, the binding cost is weighed in the same proportion as its inertia only as $`u\to0`$. At finite $`u`$, where $`1-\kappa_s`$ exceeds the remainders of (7.11), (7.12), it is weighed less, by $`\kappa_s<1`$, and $`\Gamma_{\rm pair}-\bar\Gamma=4(1-\kappa_s)g_1g_2\ell_0\tau^2\phi'/(t_f(g_1+g_2))`$. For $`\tau_1\neq\tau_2`$, $`\Gamma_b=\Gamma_i`$ for one body only at the tuned source distance $`\kappa_s=(1+\tau_i^2/\tau_j^2)/2`$, which exists iff $`\tau_i<\tau_j`$. $`\Gamma_b=\Gamma_1=\Gamma_2`$ only for $`\tau_1=\tau_2`$ and $`u\to0`$. Control: any $`u>0`$ (e.g. $`u=1/4`$).
- **(7.13)** At fixed $`n,\gamma_i,M_i,\tau_i`$: mark quantities $`\propto t_f^{-1}`$, lattice quantities $`\propto t_f^{-2/3}`$ ($`w\gg1`$), and $`D^{\rm mark}/\Delta E_0`$, $`\Gamma_b/\Gamma_i`$ are independent of $`t_f`$ at leading order.

## Consistency checks

1. **Dimensions.** $`g,\tau,t_f,M,K_0`$ are energies (rates in $`\lambda`$); $`P,r,u,\phi,\kappa_s,\delta_m`$ are dimensionless. So $`\mu,D`$ are inverse energies, and (7.3), (7.5), (7.7), (7.8) are consistent ($`M/\tau^2`$, $`\mu_1\mu_2K_0`$). $`\Gamma=E''W'`$ and $`\tau^2\phi'`$ are both energy squared per place.
2. **$`g_2\to0`$** (no binding): $`B,M_i^o,W_b',D^{\rm mark}\to0`$ linearly, (7.3) gives $`m_1\to\mu_1`$, $`F\to0`$ so $`w\to\infty`$ (S4 fails, as it must), and (7.9) is the single-body weight of (4.5).
3. **Coincident held pair $`r=0`$** ($`\langle V\rangle_0=K_0=0`$). (7.9) plus (7.10) give $`\phi'[n(g_1+g_2)+2\kappa_s(C_1^{(2)}+C_2^{(2)}+B(0))]=\phi'[nG+2\kappa_sC_G^{(2)}]`$, $`G=g_1+g_2`$, as must hold: two point couplings at one place are one coupling $`G`$.

## Open issues

- The code has $`N_f=1`$, so S5 is reached only at $`\delta_m=0.09`$ (S1) and $`0.13`$ (S2), with $`L=256`$. The remainders have sizeable coefficients: $`W_b'-(7.10)`$ is up to $`3.4\,\eta\lvert B(0)\phi'\rvert`$, and the band contract misses 5–10% of $`D_0`$. The leading form of (7.12) is reproduced to 4–12% in S1 inside the S6 hierarchy, and to 28–44% in S2. At $`u=\frac1{16}`$ the deficit $`1-\kappa_s`$ is not resolved.
- The kinetic weight $`-K_0\phi'`$ accounts for the difference between the exact selected level and the held-field average to 4–14% (S2), 9–30% (S1) and 16–45% (R); the excess grows with $`u`$ and $`\eta`$.
- The fall itself (time evolution) is not simulated; $`\Gamma`$ is taken from (5.14), (6.13) applied to the centre contract (7.14). The exact eigenvalues with the fixed source ($`L=12,16`$) confirm (7.14) but do not resolve $`W_0`$: at $`P=2\pi/L`$ the internal state already differs from $`u_0`$. $`W_0`$ is confirmed through the $`P=0`$ symbol instead. The $`b_0/m`$ form of (6.13) differs from $`W_0`$ at relative $`O(1-\beta_0)`$ in $`\langle V\rangle_0`$.
- The remainders of (7.15) are order estimates. Near the pair they are larger: at $`s=4\approx2w`$ ($`L=64`$, case A) $`W_{\rm ex}'`$ differs from $`W_0'`$ by 5.9% of $`W_b'`$, against 0.8–1.3% at $`s\ge8`$.
- The bound $`O(L^2(w^3/4)^{L/2-2})`$ on $`\rho_L`$ for $`w\lesssim1`$ is an order estimate without constants. Control F lies below it ($`\rho_L=0.125`$ at $`L=6`$; $`7.6\cdot10^{-3}`$ at $`L=8`$).
- Descriptions that violate (N×) ($`g_1+g_2`$ near 0, e.g. $`g_1=-g_2`$) would need a band contract on $`\mathcal G`$ together with the jointly flipped sector. They are outside the class.
- $`\kappa_s`$ is a property of the ring (zero mode). Other geometries are out of scope (tori).
- $`c_i^2=\tau_i^2[1+O(N_f\eta^2)]`$ is an estimate of the overlap corrections of (5.3), not derived in detail.

## Methods used

- Rayleigh–Schrödinger and Schrieffer–Wolff perturbation theory
- Bloch sectors of the joint translation, band contract
- Bloch decomposition with a fixed source, centre (Wannier) representation, symbol at $`P=0`$
- Hellmann–Feynman theorem, Perron–Frobenius
- Lattice Green function on the ring (pseudo-inverse of the Laplacian)
- Continuum limit of the relative motion (linear potential)
- Exact diagonalization, shift-invert Lanczos, Richardson extrapolation (code)
